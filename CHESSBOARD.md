`HPWorldScene._buildChessBallet` at (−40, 6), station `chess` ("The Human Chess Match").
Read with [`MODES.md`](MODES.md) and [`PROCESSIONS.md`](PROCESSIONS.md).*

---

## 1. What stands there

An eight-by-eight of light and dark squares, 1.15 m to the square, on a two-course stylobate
with a meander frieze, and thirty-two dancers on it who play three games — the *chorea
elegantissima* of the Queen's entertainment after the banquet. The pieces move in real time
through the three rounds (`this._chess` keeps the move, the round, the mover and the taken),
and the pieces the book takes off the board are taken off it.

**The liveries are the point and must stay as the book has them.** The book dresses the
**Queen of both sides in gold and the King of both sides in silver**. Russell's Hand E — the
alchemist annotating the Buffalo copy — saw at once that this is the wrong way round for the
Geberian schema (Sol gold and masculine) and read the whole match as its correction: a king
*made out of silver* wins the first two rounds, and only on the third does gold come out on
top — *'Rex ex auro factus victoriam ultimam et ultimo victor triumphat'*, then his hand
falters to *'[Re]gina', 'aura', '☉ uestita', 'victrix'*, and closes with *'Auru(m)'*
(Russell 2014, pp. 188–190; `hp.db.folio_descriptions` h1r). Fabio Chigi, in the Vatican copy,
read the same pages as theatre: *'comincia a descrivere il ballo in figura del gioco di
scacchi cosa bella'* — a *ballo* first and a game second.

So the gowns are colour-coded on purpose and the figures are kept out of the painted
cut-outs (`cutout: null`): a Botticelli card brings its own colours and the two sides became
indistinguishable.

## 2. The music that is not there

Each round is danced to music that changes tempo with the moves; the site is silent. See
[`MUSICIANS.md`](MUSICIANS.md). The dance itself carries the tempo.

## 3. Still owed

- The board is a court of its own; in the book the game is danced in the Queen's hall after
  the supper, which is now laid one station east ([`BANQUET.md`](BANQUET.md)). The two are
  the same evening and nothing yet connects them.
- Dice lie about the chess court in Roll (a litter zone), which is anachronistic to this
  game but attested in the book (Dallington 3, ours 8).
