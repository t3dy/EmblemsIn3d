# Rhizopoulou 2016, Table 1 — the 285 botanical entities of the Hypnerotomachia, with the Aldine signatures

*Captured 2026-09-07 from the publisher's full-table view of "On the botanical content of Hypnerotomachia Poliphili", Botany Letters 163(2), Table 1. The table is the author's; the scientific names are hers (nomenclature after ThePlantList); the vernacular names are Godwin's (her source text), not this project's. Signatures (a–z, A–F) are the 1499 gatherings, leaf number, ′ = verso — the same references `hp.db.page_concordance` resolves. Footnotes are the author's table footnotes, numbered as in the original; their text was not captured with the table.*

*How to read it for the world: the **first column is where the plant is in the book.** `a3` is the dark wood and the stream (ch. I); `d6′–d7′` the woods of the way to the palace; `e1′–e2` the palace gardens (ch. VII); `g3–g3′` the banquet's perfumes; `m3′` the symbolic wood; `s7′–s8′` and `t1–t8′` the Cythera plantations (chs. XXI–XXII); `u3–u6′` the herb terraces and the spice garden; `y1–y7` Polia's garland and the meadow; `z5′–z6` the bosco.*
