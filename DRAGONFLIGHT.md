# Dragon Flight — the dream from the air, and what the other modes look like from up there

*Subject brief, 2026-09-08. `src/systems/DragonFlight.js`; entered from the *Fly as the
Dragon* card. The dragon is plate 16's — the one that drives Poliphilo into the vaults under
the pyramid (chapter V). Read with [`MODES.md`](MODES.md).*

---

## 1. The controls, and the bounds

A third-person flight controller: the dragon is a rigid mount with a heading, a climb angle,
a speed and a visual bank; the camera orbits it from behind and above, and the reader owns
that camera (drag to swing, wheel to bring it in or out). **W/S** speed, **A/D** or arrows
turn, **R/F** climb and dive, **Shift** boost, **C** reset the camera, **Esc** land,
**1–9** fly to a wonder, **0** to the island. Bounds are the whole dream: x ±60, z −208 … 54,
y 0.9 … 48 — Cythera at z −150 is inside them, so the dragon crosses the sea the boat cannot
yet cross.

Landing puts the walker back under the dragon; the walker is locked while it flies.

## 2. What the world owes the dragon

From forty metres a wonder is its **roofline and its footprint**. Interiors are invisible;
coursing is invisible; a plaque is a dot. So a new station owes Fly a shape that reads from
above: a round clearing (Adonis), a pale hull with a blue flag (the exeres), coloured discs
on a chessboard (the banquet), a white tomb in a dark grove. And it owes Fly its
*colour* — aerial perspective (`RENDERING.md`) blues everything past sixty metres, and a
station that is only the sward's green vanishes into the sward.

## 3. Still owed

- The dragon does not see the litter or the collapse (Roll only), and should not.
- **No wonder is designed for the dragon** except the chessboard and Cythera's rings. The
  water-labyrinth — seven rings in water — is the one the book itself shows from a mount,
  and it is the best thing in the world to fly over; nothing marks it from the air.
- Digit 0 flies to the island's station rather than circling it; a circuit of Cythera at
  the rim's height, the twenty wedges turning underneath, would be the one scripted flight
  worth writing.

## 4. How it plays

| Mode | Relation |
|---|---|
| **Walk** | The dragon starts where the walker stood and lands there; nothing else is shared. |
| **Tour** | The tour cannot be flown; digits fly to the same stations the tour visits, but without the notes. A tour-from-the-air (the stops in order, the panel open) is possible and unwritten. |
| **Dream** | The dream walks; the dragon is the mode that lets you *not* follow the plot. |
| **Roll** | Never coexist. |
| **Layers** | None speak in flight. The station name shows. |
