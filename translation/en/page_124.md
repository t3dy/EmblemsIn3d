The flat lip of the square opening of these little boxes, or raised beds, was corniced with gold, with
the subtlest lineaments, well polished and adorned; the faces of which, of glass plates gilded within,
and with a wonderful graving of a most curious history, stood out most beautifully, enclosed within
golden channels which held them bitingly, with the lowest socle raised a sixth of a foot.

The hedge going round the garden, at a nice distance, was with bellied columns of the said material,
clothed with flower-bearing bindweed, all in relief; and here and there there stood fluted squares of
gold, and from one to the other it arched, with the required beam, and frieze, and cornice, with a due
projection above the glass capital of the round column. The solid part of which beneath the arches was
feigned jasper with a manifold colouring, meeting and bright. Which arches were somewhat raised and
drawn up from the solid in proportion; the throats of the arches were stopped with rhombs of the purest
glass, a third of a foot their diameter, between twinned little channels likewise enclosed and hedged
about with a various encaustic painting most acceptable to the senses.

The whole area besides was strewn with little glass roundings and other most fitting figurings, to the
utmost grace, with a mutual consent and a steady cohesion, with a chief gemlike shining, without any
adulterating of foliage set beneath. Above the flowers there breathed a chief fragrance from an
anointing, they being smeared and made rosy.

Most skilfully here the sweet-spoken Logistica made some little narration, and, discoursing of nature,
praised the excellent making, and the nobility of the material and the art and the invention (such as
would not be found at Murano), and blaming its nature, she said: "Poliphilo, let us climb this most
excellent watch-tower, near the garden." And Thelemia remaining below, by a winding stair we climbed
