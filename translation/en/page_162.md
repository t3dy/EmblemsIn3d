Beneath which triumphal six-horse team was the axle at the middle, in which the spokes of the wheels
were fixed, of a pomegranate-flower lineament, narrowing then at the pointed lips with a little apple
at the circumference. Which pole was of finest and weighty gold, refusing the gnawing rust and the
burning fire, the deadly poison of virtue and of peace. It was exceedingly kept by those who held
festival, leaping around with measured and sudden turnings, with the most solemn clappings, with their
garments girt with little flying bands; and those seated upon the drawing centaurs praised amorously,
with consonant voices and with sung verses and with an extreme exultation, the holy cause and the
divine mystery.
