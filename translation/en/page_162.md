## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** The trumpets, the axle, and the dancing round the first car. *Cornitibici*
as horn-blowers, *meditullo* as the hub and *liniamento Balustico* as pomegranate-flower moulding
are all right. One sentence must be fixed, because it turns a moral epigram into a description of
fire: *El quale Polo era di finissimo et ponderoso oro, repudiante el rodicabile erugine, et lo
incendioso Vulcano, della virtute et pace exitiale veneno* is rendered so that "the deadly poison of
virtue and of peace" attaches to the fire. It belongs to the **gold** — gold, which rust and fire
cannot touch, is itself the deadly poison of virtue and peace — which is a characteristic aside and
is lost.

*Confidence stated 2026-09-20 by a second reader, not by the translator (ticket tr-confidence-unstated-180).*
