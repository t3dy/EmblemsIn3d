And for such a reason the delightful and insatiable desire of the one and of the other could not firmly
agree; like a man sharpened with hunger and raging among manifold and various dishes, who, eager for
all, remains wholly content in his burning appetite with none, but infected with a ravenous hunger.

*The most beautiful Nymph, having come to Poliphilo, bearing a torch in her left hand, and with the
other free, invites him to go with her; and here Poliphilo, warmed more by the sweet love of the elegant
damsel, begins to feel his senses inflamed by it.*

Beholding present before me the real and intelligible object of a most excellent showing of so comely a
presence and divine aspect, and of a plentiful heap and universal gathering of unseen beauty and more
than human shapeliness, I reckoned small and slight for this, and unequal, all the inestimable delights
seen before, and riches and lofty magnificences, beside so great a worth as is hers.

O happy, then, is he who shall quietly possess such and so great a treasure of love. But not only a
happy possessor — truly most blessed I call him who, humbly submitting to all her desires and rule,
shall by any means be possessed and held by her. O highest Jove, behold the stamped trace of your
divine image, left in this most noble creature; whence, if Zeuxis had had her alone for his
contemplation, he would fittingly have chosen her as his one most apt exemplar, most praised above all
the girls of Agrigentum and of the whole round world, of the highest and absolute perfection.
