For already at the first cooking of the first amorous burnings, brought most sweetly into my mind (but

open, and to set wide windows to the amorous approaches and kindlings, and to be lit again by
disposition at the pleasant fires, and aptly to put itself under so great a burden.

So that already in me a familiar stirring grew stronger at the busy and inward ardours. To which I
reckoned her at present a worthier and fitter help than to the hollow little ships sailing in the swift
and tossing waves of the measured sea in ill weather is Tiphys with his tiller and most convenient
guiding, and the star of Castor. More welcome still than that of Mylitta to the beaten Adonis. And to
Aphrodite that of Peristera, her obedient Nymph. And more acceptable than the dittany of Ida, brought
by the daughter of Dione, with its purple flower, to the wound of pious Aeneas.

And feeling my breast already shaken with inward roughnesses, and silently filled again and closely
packed, and my straying thoughts gathered into it, and thinking with a busy love, the wound no longer
to be healed widened and grew. And my little spirits being drawn back into me little by little, I
almost dared to assure myself of showing her, and expressing, my intense burnings and amorous
thoughts. Then, wholly lost in a blind desire — for which cause I could no longer refuse the invading
approaches, nor resist the burning boilings — I cried out with a stirred and full voice and said:

"O delicate and divine damsel, whoever you are, use less strong torches to burn me and to consume my
sad heart. By now it burns all over with an unceasing and goading fire, and I feel a pointed and
sharpest and flaming dart pierce and go through the middle of my soul."
