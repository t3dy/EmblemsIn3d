Where there breathed a most welcome breath of heaven, with unchanging airs, in this meadow flowering
round about, founded and abiding in a wide and circling space, with bending shrubs of every fruit,
sweet to the taste, and packed with every health, with a perpetual greenness, with the ordering of a
ruled arrangement for comeliness and charm and adornment, disposed and planted, brought wonderfully to
perfection by the chief study of nature, and unceasingly lit by the most precious gold.

Logistica being silent, then, both of them holding me by the hands, we went out festively through the
gap, or opening, of one of the arches, outside the girdle of the ivied enclosure; and going on from
there through the middle of that compass, Thelemia saying most joyfully: "Let us go now to the
appointed doors." So that, going forward through the pleasant region and country with a ready and
prepared advance, I beheld the sky purged of every darkening cloud, with sweet, witty and particular
discoursings.

I, being unsatisfied in understanding the whole of the inestimable riches, unconsiderable delights, and
unmatchable treasure (to which let Osiris yield, the builder of the two temples of gold, one to
heavenly Jove and the other to the royal) of the most sacred Queen, put to them this little question:

"Tell me, blessed young women, if my curious asking be welcome to you now. Among all the precious
little stones that I have been able to see clearly, I judged one of great worth and price — nay,
incomparable and most precious beyond esteeming. Much and far more was it than the jasper that had
stamped upon it the likeness of Nero in his breastplate. Nor such either was the flashing topaz of the
statue of Arsinoe, the Arabian queen. Nor was so great a cost laid out for the gem for which Nonius the
senator was proscribed, as for that shining and incomparable diamond, of so unseen a beauty and
thickness, that hung from the richest necklace above the snowy breast of our divine Queen. What carving
was that? For so great was its flashing, and being besides at a distance, I could not perfectly discern
it. So that this only remains, which holds me anxious and my mind in suspense to know."
