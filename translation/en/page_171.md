## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** Two short panels: Jove handing the infant to Mercury for the nymphs to
nurse, and Cupid's arrow drawing Jove down to a mortal girl. Accurately rendered, and *talaricato et
caducifero* is correctly the winged sandals and the herald's staff, though Mercury is not named.
Medium because the page is only a few lines, so the judgement rests on a small sample, and because
it breaks off in the middle of the sentence describing Cupid's suit against his mother, which is
completed on page 172.
