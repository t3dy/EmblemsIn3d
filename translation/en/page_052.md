At the one and the other extremity of the bronze Tablet were two boys holding it, or winged little
spirits, perfectly formed, in such a manner that the diligent statuary of the famous burden-bearing
boys of the Ravenna Snail-stair never saw such an exemplar. Joining their swelling little hands to it,
they held it readily, naked, of the said metal. Aptly carried upon a flat of Cyanean stone, more to
the grace of its colouring than that which, compacted in little cakes, is forced to the vomiting-out
of the perfect azure; and it shone with a glassy lustre.

In the front of the Zophorus stretched above the porphyry columns were Spoils of Breastplates,
Corslets knotted of triple-threaded rings, Bucklers, Helmets, Bundles, Axes, Torches, Quivers, Darts
and many other engines of war — no less Airy than maritime and terrestrial — of most worthy making;
and thus at their Haunches they showed without doubt the victories, the power and the triumphs which
made high-thundering Jove resound, and mortals perish in sweetness.

Then in due order followed the wickedly fine Cornice, of such lineaments as decently concur with so
great an elegance of work. For otherwise, just as in the human body one quality discordant from
another brings on sickness — because fitness is not found in the friendship of the composition — and,
the accidents not being neatly distributed to their due place, deformity follows: so, neither more nor
less, is that fabric dissonant and infirm where due harmony and measured order are not found. Which
thing the modern unlearned confound, being ignorant of the placing distribution. Wherefore our most
wise master likens the building to a human body well proportioned in its sharing and decently clothed.

Beyond this cornice, with reversed stepping, four squarings presided: two standing above the order of
the fluted, or Caryatid, Columns, and two drawn in. In the middle division of the two remembered, there
sat a Nymph of excellent relief of brass, holding two torches: one quenched, turned towards the heavy
earth, and one lit, towards the Sun. The burning one in the right, and the other in the left hand.
