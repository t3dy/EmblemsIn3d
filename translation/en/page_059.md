Insatiably, then, considering now one and now another most beautiful and massive work, I said silently:
if the fragments of holy antiquity, and its breakages and ruin, and in a manner its very Chippings,
lead us into stupendous admiration and to so much delight in looking at them, what would its
completeness do?

And so I thought too within myself, reasonably: perhaps in the inner shrines is the venerable Altar of
the mysterious sacrifices and sacred flames, or else the statue of the Divine Venus, or her most holy
Aphrodisium, and that of her bow-bearing and arrow-shooting little son. And with devout veneration,
the right foot set upon the sacred threshold, there ran before me a fleeing and white Mouse.

At once, without other curious thought, with searching eyes I entered somewhat into the open space and
the lighted entry, and things worthy of eternal regard presented themselves to me. Here on the right
and left side the panelled wall was of most polished marbles. In the middle part of which levelling
was fitted a great roundness, enclosed in the circuit of a leafy jewel with excellent company of
carving. Which (like the other opposite it) was of the blackest stone, contemptuous of hard iron, of a
mirroring brightness.

Between which, passing (unaware of them), I was invaded by a sudden fear at my own image.
Nevertheless I was tempered again by an unlooked-for pleasure. Because in them there was clearly
offered the judgment of the histories notably painted in mosaic work. And on both the lower parts,
beneath the shining mirrors, were lengthwise seats of stone. The pavement clean and free of dust,
worked in new and gracious pottery-work. And so too the coloured soffit was free of the wicked
