Which expressions, according to the requirement of the colourings, had their veins so naturally
accessory and fitting to the maker's determined devising of the precious stone; and in these little
images, however small, nevertheless no defect at all showed itself, even in the least parts. But every
part was seen distinctly perfect.

Out of the vessel aforesaid there sprouted a leafy vine of gold with curled vine-leaves, fruitfully
adorned with little clusters, with crimson grains of Indian amethyst, and the foliage of the holy green
selenite of Persia, not subject to the movements of the moon, and placid to Cupid. Keeping its bearer
safe; and it roofed the six-horse team.

At each corner of the flat of the triumphal car there shone widely, set there, a most skilfully made
candelabrum, founded upon three little horned feet, of branching coral, excellent for country folk.
Lightnings. Whirlwinds. And driving off tempests, and kindly to its bearer, and an amulet. Such a like
was not found by Perseus beneath the Gorgon's head, nor such in the Red Sea, nor such in the Persian,
nor such the Drepanic.
