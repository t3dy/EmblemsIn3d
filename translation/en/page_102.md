# Page 102 — Chapter IX

The crown above the throne, in encaustic painting, held a comely beardless image with flowing yellow
hair. With a little of the breast covered by a scant cloth, above the spread wings of an eagle, which
with head raised contemplated it fixedly. Which was wreathed with an azure diadem, adorned with seven
rays; and at the eagle's feet a branch here and a branch there of green and undying laurel, and such
signs, one to each side, were expressed. In the like manner I saw painted in every crown, that is, the
figure proper to its planet.

It fell out by chance that behind my shoulders was the jewel holding the history of wing-footed
Mercury; and, turning, I saw how his kindness was corrupted when he found himself in the malignant tail
of the venomous Scorpion. And straightening again I thought how, in a most abject habit, I found myself
among so many sumptuous garments — neither more nor less than that deadly beast, misshapen and vile
among the most noble signs of the zodiac.

Then they sat in an ordered way upon the magnificent benches. Which went round the whole circuit of the
walls. Now on the right side and the left of the court, as many as could aptly sit, the noble ladies
most richly adorned, and with unusual and elegant dressings of the head, with womanly devices, their
hair drawn back and plaited in distinct little knots, the fairest in the world. Some with the fair head
curled in Poppaean ringlets, justly parted and modestly waved above the rosy and shining foreheads and
the smooth temples. And others with hair (like obsidian, not of Latium, not of Spain, but of India) of
the deepest black, comelily adorned with the whitest pearls, and with clasps of incredible price
ornamented at their snowy shoulders.

They stood with so much reverence and attention that all at one time, when the servers at the tables
made their reverences kneeling, these did the same, rising from their pleasant seats. Each clothed in
shining gold, with marvellous weaving and workmanship. These did not eat at these tables.
