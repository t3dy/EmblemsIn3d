In the pendant towards the fork of her whitening breast, in the middle between two great pearls, was
threaded a flashing ruby, most round; beyond the pearls on either side there followed two blazing
sapphires, and then again two eastern pearls. Beyond which, here and there, there followed two
lightning emeralds, and again two pearls, and then two most shining jacinths. All these gems of a
pill-like form, most just, and of the thickness of a berry, with an excellent and friendly parting.

Her most blonde head, with her hair spread out and loose, poured over her gracious neck, appeared
plentiful with curling and shining little locks, seen no otherwise than as the finest threads of gold,
unsteadily glowing. The parted hair of her crown, pressed down by a wreath of fragrant amethyst
violets, hanging a little above her festive forehead, made a delightful triangular parting, such as was
ever vowed to a Genius. And from beneath the little fillet there came out in an ordered way her
vine-tendrilled hair, part of it quivering and shadowing her fair temples, not hiding her smallest
ears, more beautiful than were ever dedicated to Memory.

Thence then the remainder of her yellow hair, spread out behind her sparkling neck, and hanging poured
from her round shoulders, restlessly stretching over her shapely back beyond the turning of her knees,
and moderately fanning itself in little waves — so that Juno's bird does not so lovelily spread its
eyed feathers; such hair Berenice did not vow for her Ptolemy in the temple of Venus, nor did Conon the
mathematician see such set in a triangle.

In her glad forehead too, beneath two fine, blackest, half-circled and parted brows (such as were
perhaps never seen in Ethiopia among the Abyssinians, nor had Juno ever such in her keeping), there
shone two festive and radiant eyes. Enough to pour Jove down in a rain of gold; ready with a clear
light, with the dark iris covered by the milky white; near which her purpling cheeks, with the round
and laughing dimples of two little hollows, comelily adorned with a choice grace. They breathed the
colour of fresh roses, gathered at the rising of Aurora. And then set in vessels of the cleanest
crystal of Cyprus, shining through no otherwise, with a reddening clearness: so, without fail, with such
brightness I judged them.
