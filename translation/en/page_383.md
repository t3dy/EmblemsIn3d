## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** Polia's **genealogy and the founding of Treviso**: her ancestor **Lelio Syliro**, sent as
Consul to the March of **Taurisana** (Treviso), marries **Trivisia Calardia Pia**, dowered with the Venetian
countryside; their eleven children (five named sons, six named daughters) are so beautiful the family **compares them
to Venus** — a hubris (like Atalanta) that will bring divine punishment. All secure; the historical/legendary frame of
Polia's family.

- **The founding of Treviso.** Colonna roots Polia's lineage in the real city of **Treviso** (Tarvisium), named from
  its mountain, settled by the Roman consul Lelio Syliro — tying the dream-romance to an actual North-Italian place.
  This has long fed the theory that the author (the acrostic names "Francesco Colonna") was a Trevisan; the passage is
  the book's strongest geographic anchor. (Nothing in the 3D world uses it, but it grounds Polia.)
- **the hubris that dooms the family.** The Lelii's fatal error: they credit their daughters' god-given beauty to
  "their own virtue" and let the daughters **compare themselves to Venus** — the classic *hybris* (Atalanta,
  Arachne, Niobe) that provokes the goddess. This sets up the plague and the vow to Diana that drive Polia's own
  story (the argument, p. 150 of NOTES: "Polia's plague, her vow to Diana"). The cruelty Polia confessed (p. 382) is
  the fruit of this ancestral pride.
- **the Roman marriage-rite.** *Confarreatio* (the patrician wedding), the "Herculean knot" (the bride's girdle),
  Cinxia and Zygia Lucina (goddesses of the girdle and of marriage/childbirth): Colonna gives Polia's parents an
  authentic Roman nuptial, his antiquarian precision now serving the family-history. The register stays learned even
  in Polia's plainer voice.
- **the eleven children named** — a full family roster (Maurio, Halcyoneo, Tipula, Narbonio, Musilistre; Murgania,
  Quintia, Septimia, Alimbrica, Astorgia, Melmia): Polia situates herself among siblings, giving the narrative a real
  household. Her own name and place in it emerge as the account proceeds.
