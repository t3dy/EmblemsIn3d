And having put it in hers, I felt her press it between warm snow and milky curd. And it seemed to me —
nay, so it was — that I was touching and handling something other than a thing of human condition.
Whence, after I had done so, I remained wholly stirred and shaken and suspicious, not understanding
things unvisited by mortals. Nor yet what ought to follow from it, with my common ragged dress and with
my foolish and vulgar manners, judging myself misshapen beside her, unfit and unlike such a fellowship,
and that it was unlawful for a mortal and earth-born man to enjoy such delights.

For which cause, my face blushing, filled all with a bashful wonder, and grieving somewhat at my own
lowness, I made myself her follower.

At last, though not with my whole and wholly recovered mind, I began to bring back my frightened and
troubled spirits, persuading myself that I was deservedly at a most blessed outcome, beside such a
beautiful and divine object, and in such a place. Whose excellent aspect would have had force enough to
draw and carry the lost souls out of the eternal flames, and to bring the bodies not yet joined in
their tombs back to their partner; and Bacchus would have neglected the famous drunkenness of Gauranum,
