Others courteously, opening the closely fastened bosoms of their gowns, adding flowers to flowers, put
within them the roses stripped of their petals, the sucking kiss following after. And at one time they
struck each other with a hand that did not hurt, without weal or bruise, giving each other slaps sweetly
upon their dimpled cheeks, faintly reddened as shining Phoebus shows himself in the wheels of the cool
dawn, with the newest and most unthought-of battles that ever Love knew how to feign.

All festive and cheerful, and all provoked to delights. With girlish gestures and movements and a
maidenly simplicity, tangled in a sincere love without offence to honoured virtue. Free and exempt from
any sad encounter, and from the rivalry of skin-changing fortune.

Beneath the tempered shades let down by the weeping sisters of wicked Phaethon, and by the undying
Daphne, and by tufted pines, with fine and pointed fronds, and by the tree dyed again with the burning
blood of the unhappy Babylonians, and by the straight cypresses and the greenest orange and cedar
trees, and by other most notable and most leafy trees, most fruitful of flowers and fruits, with an
eternal greenness, most beautiful beyond esteeming and fragrant. Which, set in order upon the gracious
banks and dispensed through the plain, at a moderate distance and interval, took up the grassy earth,
full and clothed with the green periwinkle with its sky-blue flowers.

Alas, then, what heart would be so cold and frozen that it should not be excitedly kindled, seeing
present before it such real and delightful offices of an even and answering love? So I reasonably
suspected that even Diana the huntress would wholly and easily have been set on fire. And the icy
Helice pursued by her.

For which cause I should almost have dared to utter a foolishness: that the spirits below suffer no
other torment than the envy they have of these. Who without end live happily in delights and triumph,
with the utmost pleasure, with no weariness of things present nor any surfeit of them.

Whence more than once my heart, kindled through my eyes with an extreme sweetness, and greatly burning,
my amorous soul went out as far as my hesitating mouth. And keeping my mind fixed upon those delightful
pleasures, and the soft kisses, and the abundant rewards of winged Cupid, looking on with a curious
gaze, it certainly seemed to me in that space to feel of that kindled soul that this was its passing,
and that it was sweetly departing to the last and utmost bounds of blessedness.
