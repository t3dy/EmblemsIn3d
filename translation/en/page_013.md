it seemed that I was in a spacious plain, which showed itself all green and much adorned, painted
variously with manifold flowers. And with kindly airs there was there a certain silence. Nor yet did
any noise, nor any formed voice, come to ears most ready to hear. But with the gracious rays of the
Sun the temperate season passed.

In which place I, wandering with timid wonder, said within myself: here no humanity appears to the
desiring gaze, nor yet any wild thing of the woods, nor forest-dweller, nor creature of the glade,
nor any tame beast. No country house, no cottage of the fields, no shepherd's roof, no hut nor
shieling is to be seen. Nor likewise, in these grassy places, did I see any keeper of sheep, nor
goatherd, nor oxherd, nor groom, nor any straying flock and herd, with their rustic two-mouthed
Syrinx, nor with their sounding pipes of bark. But confident, because of the quiet shore and the
kindliness of the place, and going forward almost made secure, I looked this way and that, and saw
the tender leaves resting unmoved, discerning no other work.

And so, straight before me, I turned my unknowing journey towards a thick wood. Into which, having
entered somewhat, I did not perceive that I had thus incautiously left — I know not in what manner —
the path itself. Whereupon a sudden fear at once invaded my suspended heart, spreading itself through
my pale limbs with a quickened beating, my cheeks become bloodless of their colour. Seeing that to my
eyes no trace whatever was granted to be seen, nor any turning aside. But in that thorny wood there
appeared only close thickets, pricking brakes, the woodland Ash that is hateful to vipers, rough Elms
grateful to the fruitful vine, the corky Cork-oak fit for a woman's adding, hard Turkey-oaks, strong
oaks, and acorn-bearing Oaks and Holm-oaks, and so abounding in branches that they did not permit the
rays of the gracious Sun to reach the dewy ground entire. But, as beneath a vaulted roof covered over
with thickening leaves, the kindly light did not penetrate. And in this manner I found myself in the
cool shade, the humid air, and the dusky wood.
