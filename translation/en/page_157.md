And most of these distinguished Nymphs had their blonde hair gathered together, with exquisite little
twists in three parts, and knotted with a delightful weaving. Others spread their unsteady and restless
tresses loosely, poured behind their milky necks. Some with their most abundant hair wrapped in the
thinnest veils, their glad brow left graciously shadowed with twisted hair. And so, without labour,
mistress nature gave them no ordinary grace.

And with fillets woven of threads of gold with a border of sparkling pearls; and others had adorned
their haired heads with richest and delightful bands. And at their straight throats the most sumptuous
necklaces, and precious collars, and armlets, and clasps; and their small ears most beautifully hung
with various jewels. And the dignity of the hair with a clean and notable adornment, the brow encircled
with a threading of great and roundest pearls. All which most excellent things, together with their
most elegant persons, would easily have altered any rustic, fierce, obstinate and inhuman heart.

Then they showed delightfully their whitening breasts, uncovered as far as the round little swellings.
Then the maidenly little body upon straight legs, with small feet. And some bare upon antique sandals,
held with little cords of gold between the great toe and the middle one, and near the least, and about
the full heel; and thence they came together neatly upon the top of the foot in an artful lacing. Some
tightly shod, and with little golden hooks and loops. Some with soled buskins of purple and of other
joyful colours, such as Caius Caligula never first wore. Others with fitted high boots girt above their
white and fleshy calves; and some with little slippers, with masterly little loops of gold and of silk;
many with the ancient Sicyonian shoes, and some with choice silken pumps and with golden clasps most
seemly set with gems.

And besides, at their neat heads, their free brows bound about with flying veils of a spider's making;
and with their biting and festive eyes, brighter than shining stars in the purest sky, beneath their
fine and circled brows. And the small nose between their apple-round cheeks, reddened like those apples
in autumn, duly with their laughing little hollows, or dimples. And their front teeth and eye-teeth,
with the others set in order, small, of the colour of refined silver, between their moist lips like the
finest coral.
