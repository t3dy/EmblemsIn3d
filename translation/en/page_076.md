With gilded borders, their shoes over the snowy ankles with a curved opening, bound tightly and

They then, becoming aware of me, stayed their Nymph-like step and stood, emptied of their sweet song,
suddenly invaded by this novelty of me newly come into that place. And marvelling to one another and
curious, silently searching me out, it appeared to them insolent and unaccustomed that in that famous
country a man foreign and strange should have arrived so by chance.

For which cause they stood for a little space among themselves, one to another with a secret
murmuring, and many times bending to look me over searchingly. As though I had been a phantom — alas, I
felt at that point all my bowels shaken, like leaves of Sweet-flag quivering in the impetuous winds. For
scarcely being reassured from the terror often spoken of, and judging at once and deservedly that they
had in them something beyond the human condition — knowing nothing else — I feared such a divine vision
as appeared to ashen Semele, deceived by the counterfeited form of Beroë of Epidaurus.
