At so imperial and lofty a sight, then, worshipping and kneeling to the ground — without delay the
chamber-women and the ladies of the court all rose from their quiet sitting, provoked by such a novelty
and spectacle, marvelling exceedingly that I should have come to such a place. But much more did I feel
my sad heart open itself restlessly, anxiously thinking over things past and things present, beset and
full of a deep astonishment. Wholly suffused with a reverent fear and an honest bashfulness.

At which novelty the seated ladies called my companions to their ears, curious. And asking under their
breath who I might be, and my strange and unlooked-for case besides. And for this the drawn eyes of all
were fixed and directed intently upon me.

Finding myself, then, most humble before this most excellent and such a presence, I remained wholly
astounded, and as it were without breath and abashed. And being asked by her the course and manner of
my coming, and my entry into that place by my companions, they readily set the whole out and fully
recited it. Moved by this, the honeyed Queen made me rise, and having heard my name, began thus
