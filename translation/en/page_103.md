Now in an instant there were set out tripods of ebony, and tables for the time, without tumult or
noise, but each server most ready for her appointed office, and eager and clear-sighted with an
affectionate effort, and wholly intent upon the service enjoined and laid upon her.

And here first of all, before the Queen, tripods of such a making were set fast. Upon a rounding of the
best jasper, distinguishedly lined. Above which were fixed three stems. The lowest part of which ended
upon the flat in a swift lion's paw of gold, engulfing the shafts with exquisite foliage, and clothed
round about with the most polished foliage. And in the middle of each was fitted a little boy's head,
held in the midst of two spread wings, from which there hung, from the one towards the other, a little
bundle of branches heaped together, swelling in the middle, filled with various fruits. Which shafts in
their topmost part had a projection most apt to bear up the round table before the Queen. This
framework was unchanging, but the sudden round tables varied. As the material of the vessels varied at
every change of table.

Then in the blink of an eye there was set upon this tripod a round table of gold, most level, three
feet in diameter and a thumb's breadth thick; and of this form and measure were all that followed. And
upon the ivory tables was spread a scented cloth of green silk, neatly stretched out, broad and long
almost down to the pavement, unravelled at the edge all round, with its own threads twisted and mingled
with threads of gold and silver, hanging beneath a band, or border, a sixth of a foot in its breadth,
