Not hindered nor taken up by any growing shrub. But all the flowery ground was one level meadow of
fragrant herbs and flower-bearing with flowers, endless of every colouring, and most beautiful of
manifold form, and sweetest of scent, as much as could ever be said. Which did not fear the burning
invasions of Phoebus, since he does not run in this most pleasant place with his swiftest horses to the
last west. But the air always purest, and every hour unveiled of clouding vapours, but eternally a
clear and unchanging day; and the earth continually grass-bearing, springlike and fragrant with
flowers, like a gracious and choice painting, standing always unhurt with its dewy freshness, and with
its colour without the interdict of time.

Here the four kinds of violet, cowslip, melilot, anemones, cornflowers, nigella, cyclamen, crowfoot,
columbine, lily of the valley and amaranth, cassidony, spikenard, Celtic nard, ambrosia, amaranth, wild
mint, citron and clove basil, and the other smallest ones; and many other fragrant and flower-bearing
herbs; all the kinds of gillyflower and the smallest Persian rose-trees, fruitful with fragrant little
roses, and hundred-leaved, and of all colourings. And countless others, with all the fragrant and
notable herbs, without any human labour excellently distributed for beauty by excellent nature herself,
and untilled they stood, with their flowering greenness never falling, and with an unfailing
pleasantness and delight. And to the utmost gratifying of the senses.

Here, then, among the notable and seemly girls, with an approved comeliness, I saw Callisto of Arcadia,
daughter of Lycaon, with the unrecognized Diana; Antiope of Lesbos with the honoured satyr, daughter of
Nycteus, from whom were born Amphion the musician and Zethus the countryman; Issa, daughter of
Macareus, with the dear shepherd. And Antichia, daughter of Accus. And the young Canace. And also her
born of Athasius. And Asteria, born of the Titan Coeus. And likewise Alcmena, gladly playing with her
counterfeited husband.

Then in turn I looked upon delightful Aegina, taking the highest pleasure both in the bright river and
