All which trees were not of a thick planting, but dispensed at the required distance, and all duly
distributed in a fitting place and aspect, greatly delightful to the eyes, and springlike with leaves.
The country-born Nymphs frequented there, and the wandering dryads, their nimble little bodies girt
with soft and twistable fronds, and their leaping hair above their wide brows; together with the horned
fauns crowned with the hollow reed, and with pithy fennel-stalk, and girt with sharp pine, with the
leaping wanton and swift satyrs, keeping solemnly the faun-feasts, come out of this pleasant and
venerable precinct, with more tender, green and new fronds than that grove of the goddess Feronia put
forth when the dwellers wished, because of the fire, to carry away her image.

We entered, then, where there were measured square spaces hedged about with the bounds of wide,
straight, four-way roads, a pace high, of dog-thorn, or thorny grape, and of ground juniper, and most
thickly bound to a wall-like level of most even box, enclosing the squares of the flowering and dewy
meadows.

In the order of the said hedges I beheld planted symmetrically the lofty victorious palms, with their
fruitful clusters of dates hanging out of their sheaths: some black, some crimson, many yellow, such as
would not be found in dewy Egypt. And perhaps the Dabulan of the tent-dwelling Arabs is not so choice,
and perhaps Jericho does not bring forth such. Which stood alternating with the greenest citrons and
oranges, hawthorn-medlars, pistachios, pomegranates, quinces, tree-myrtles, and medlars, and
service-trees, and many other noble fruit trees, most adorned with fruitfulness, in the fields as if
newly come into the spring.

Here upon the greenness of the flowering meadows, and in the cool shades, in a gathered multitude, I saw
a great troop of unaccustomed and rarely seen people of both kinds, glad together, clothed rustically in
skins, some of the fawn with white spots, dappled and painted, and others of lynxes and of leopards,
