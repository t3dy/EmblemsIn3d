Here, fervently touched with pleasant ardours, they began little by little to grow and to warm the cold
fear, and to fit the altered heat by disposition to a sincere love. And being already almost overcome
and vanquished in no ordinary measure by the kindling and inward appetite, thinking silently within
myself, I argued in various ways: O most happy above any lover whoever should be joined and made a
sharer in the love of this woman, if not wholly, at least in some part.

Then, blaming my own wicked desires, I set against them, saying: O, it would scarcely be given me to
believe that such Nymphs would deign to be with unequal and earthly men, unlike them in all things. But
without hesitating: this one is worthy of the close embraces of the gods above, and of drawing them,
stripped of their divine forms, changed and disguised, down from the high heavens to her delightful
love.

On the other side I comforted myself, that, offering her my loving soul (having no other worthier
gift), however divine she be, perhaps she will not despise it. As Artaxerxes, king of the Persians,
bent down to the water presented in the hands to drink. And for this, with lukewarm sighs, I felt the
inmost parts of my invaded breast stirred and greatly moved. Which willingly fitted itself to such a
service, and prepared itself to be kindled more easily than a dry reed-bed to the blowing east winds,
the small spark being set to it, which at the beginning begins to catch impetuously, multiplying, and
then all takes fire.

By this likeness I most abundantly felt the growth of a pleasant and familiar little flame spreading in
the prepared subject, in such a way that her amorous glances were by now to me deadly and mortal
strokes, as the flashing thunder splits the strong oaks with a sudden onset, breaking out. And I no
longer dared to look at her shining eyes, for whenever I looked at her (forced by the incredible beauty
of her most welcome aspect), and if by chance her radiant eyes had met mutually with mine, for some
little while all things seemed to me doubled, before the wavering blinking of my eyes and their former
light were restored.
