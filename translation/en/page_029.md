And the Giants, their armour cast off, closely embracing one another; some carried along with their
feet caught in the trappings; others crushed beneath their own bodies; and some falling headlong with
their wounded horses. Some, laid flat upon the ground on their backs, fought protecting themselves
with the shield. Many girt with side-swords and with belted blades, and with antique Persian
broadswords and manifold instruments of deadly shaping. The greater part on foot, fighting confusedly
with darts and bucklers. Some in corslets and helmets, marked with varied crests; and others naked,
showing that they attacked with a lively heart, intent upon death. Some breastplated, adorned with
various and most noble military ornaments. Many with the fearful look of men crying out; some with
the semblance of obstinacy and fury.

Those who were about to die, with a thread rivalling nature, expressed the effect; and others dead,
with hateful and manifold engines of war and slaughter. They showed the robust limbs and the swelling
muscles, and gave the eyes to see the office of the bones, and the hollows where the hard sinews
drew. Which conflict and duel appeared so fearful and horrible that you would say it was bloody and
arms-mighty Mars himself about to fight in single combat with Porphyrion and Alcyoneus; and the
flight they had at the braying of the ass came into my memory to see.

All these images exceeded the natural tallness and stature, and the carving was in deep relief of
most illustrious and polishable marble, the flat space between introduced of the blackest stone for
the comeliness and grace of the white stone and for the setting-off of the statuary work; and they
stood out perfectly. Here, then, were infinite tall bodies, last strivings, intent acts, breastplated
habits, and various death, with doubtful victory. Alas, my wearied spirits, and my understanding
confused by such continual variety, and my disordered senses, cannot fitly bear not only to tell the
whole, but they are not able to express even a part, with completeness, of so polished a
stone-carving.
