Therefore in this (as I so call it) Altar, deepened with gorge-mouldings covered with leaves, with a
modest stepping of fine cuttings — among which, of transparent Alabaster stone, the proportioned bands
remained untouched, going round by the extremity of the four-cornered front, equal-sided — there was
carved with the greatest diligence a man of an age passing beyond manhood, robust with rusticity, with
a thick beard of hair curled by hardness at the chin, its growth coming ill through the hard skin.

He sat upon a feigned rock, with a goat's skin, which, flayed at the hinder parts, he had girt about
him in a knot over his flanks; and the part of the neck, with the hair turned towards him, hung
between his knotted shins. Before him, in the middle of his swelling calves, was an Anvil, fixed in a
brawny piece of a truncated tree. Upon which, intent, he was forging a pair of glowing little wings,
holding the hammer raised and striking his work.

And there before him stood a most noble Matron, at whose delicate shoulders were set two wings of
feathered plumage. Who held a child, her son, naked, sitting with his little haunches upon the fleshy
thigh of his mother — the Mother Goddess holding him somewhat raised, with her bared foot set upon a
rock, held together with the seat of the hammering smith, feigned in a little hill of stone; with a
little furnace in a small cavern in which the coal-red fire burned.

And the Matron had her tresses composedly carried back over her broad brow, adorning her copious head
round about, so delicately expressed that I do not know by what reason those standing statues were not
stirred to her, who were likewise found at the smith's work.

There was moreover there an armed man of raging semblance, clothed in an antique aegis-breastplate,
with the fearful head of Medusa on the breast, and with other noble and exquisite breast-pieces; with
the baldric across the ample breast, and he held with his muscular arm a spear somewhat raised. And
with a crested helmet with its peak he had his head defended; the other arm not appearing, hindered by
the figures in front. There appeared also a young man, seen from the breast upward, clothed in thin
cloth, beyond the bowed head of the said smith.
