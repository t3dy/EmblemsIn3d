Great fragments in many parts of plane rounding, and of Ophite and of Porphyry, and of a Coralline
colour, and of many other pleasing colourings. Fragments of various storied work in high relief and
half relief, in finished and in half-finished carving; declaring its excellence, which unfailingly
accuses our own times, and shows that of such an art the perfection is fallen asleep.

Having come therefore near the middle front of that great and illustrious work, I saw an entire
portal, wonderful and conspicuous, and proportioned to the whole building. Which fabric I saw
continuous, set between the one and the other of the mountains with their sheared-off slopes, so that
I could reasonably conjecture its dimension to be twenty paces and six stadia. The joining of these
mountains was made level perpendicularly from the summit right down to the floor.

For which cause I stood pondering within myself with what iron instruments, and with how much wearing
of men's hands, and what numbers of them, so great an artifice had been forcibly brought to pass —
so incredibly laborious, and with so great a grinding away of time. Here, then, with the one mountain
and the other, this admirable structure joined itself in knowing adhesion. By which joining, as is
said above, the valley was fortified with a closure, so that no one could go out from there, or turn
back, or enter, save through this open gate.

Now above this so huge work of building — whose height from the topmost cornices to the footing and
base could easily be conjectured at a fifth of a stadium — there was founded an adamantinely
pinnacled and most portentous Pyramid. Whereupon I reasonably judged that not without inestimable
expense, and time, and the greatest multitude of mortals, could such an incredible artifice ever have
been devised and set up. And if I rightly judged its excess, beyond belief, to be a thing
unimaginable to look upon — which in the beholding wearied the visual power not a little, and thinned
the other spiritual senses — how much more to make it? Therefore, so far as the grasp of my
understanding will grant it me, in this manner I now briefly describe it.
