# Page 166 — Chapter XIV

Upon this proud and triumphal carriage I saw a whitest swan, in the amorous embraces of a renowned
Nymph, daughter of Theseus, of an incredible beauty formed; and kissing her with his divine beak, his
wings let down, he covered the uncovered parts of the noble lady; and with divine and delightful
pleasures they stood, both joined together most joyfully; and the divine swan set between her delicate
and snowy thighs.

Who sat comfortably upon two cushions of cloth of gold, exquisitely stuffed with soft down, with all
the sumptuous and adorning accessories fitting. And she clothed in a fine nymphlike gown, of whitest
silk woven with a warp of gold, shining, with an elegant ornament of precious stones in the fitting
places. Without lack of anything whatever that comelily comes together for the increase of delight.
Exceedingly notable and delightful to those who looked. With all the parts that were described at the
first, of praise and applause.
