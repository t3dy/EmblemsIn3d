In the middle centre of the level of the said square, out of an antique foliage, there sprang a most
beautiful ancient vessel, its circuit not exceeding the compass of the square flat beneath it. And
this, with all the work that follows, with every proportioned inquiry both of the rise, the breadth and
the thickness, and with the most seemly lineaments of vessels, was most diligently filed and finished,
with a perfect and polished shaping.

Which, at its pressed-in mouth, brought forth a basin. The circuit of which exceeded the base vessel
beneath, adorned round about with little channels, and with a wide mouth and broad lips, such as no
chaser ever had power to work with the graving tool.

At the central point of which another vessel stood out, wonderful for its incredible making; in the
lower third of which were slices swelling on the outside, and then there girt it round an order of
various and inestimable gems set like navels, most brilliantly adorned with a mutual alternation of the
colouring. Above this was fitted a monstrous manly head. From which there went, here and there, its
covering in exquisite foliage, clothing it all over at the meeting with the head opposite, beautifully
adorning the free body of the vessel.

At the projection of the lip, perpendicular above the head, sat a ring, from which there hung, here and
there, a little bundle with heaped-up small branches of fronds and flowers and fruits, swelling in its
curve, with a well-polished knotting, both in the one ring and in the other.
