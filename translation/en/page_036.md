And when I came to the heart, I saw, reading, how of love the little sighs are engendered, and where
love grievously wounds. And there, wholly moved, I drew from the depth of my heart a bellowing sigh,
invoking Polia; so that I felt the whole brazen machine resound, with no little horror. An art
invented above every exquisite thing, that a man should make himself without an excelling anatomy.

O illustrious wits of the past! O truly golden age, when virtue kept concert with fortune! To this
age alone you left ignorance and rival avarice as an inheritance.

I saw, standing out in another part of the aforesaid thickness, the brow of a woman's head somewhat
uncovered among the ruins, the rest buried by the greatest breakages. For which cause, judging a like
workmanship to be there, and fearing the disordered and unequal ruins, I left off looking at it and
returned to the first place.

Where also, not too far distant from the great horse, there offered itself at the level a very great
Elephant of blackening stone, more than Obsidian, sparkling with gold and with silvery flecks
copiously scattered like a fine dust, and glittering through the stone. The stubborn hardness of which
plainly showed its clear lustre; for in it every object represented itself and was sent back most
properly to that quarter — except where the metal had spread its greenish rust.

And this fittingly, because on the summit of its most ample back it had a marvellous Bronze
saddle-cloth, with two tight Girths carried round the monstrous bulk. Between which very great
bindings, fastened with buckles of the same stone, there was held a quadrangle answering to the
thickness of the Obelisk set above; so that no perpendicular of weight should have air, or rather
void, beneath it. For being hollow within, it is not solid, nor lasting.

Which quadrangular part was beautifully lined on each of its three faces with Egyptian characters.
