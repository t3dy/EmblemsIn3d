Thus, then, by a sharpening love painfully hurt and afflicted, wavering, we came at length to the right
part of the spacious field.

In this place there were set in order the greening trees, plentifully leafy, full of most sweet
flowers, fruit-bearing about the site, and with manifold kinds of foliage and of undying greenness;
they gladdened the heart of those who looked upon them.

The Nymph beloved of Venus stood still, and stayed there, and I with her. Where, looking with an almost
half-power of sight — because it could not be wholly moved away from its amorous object — by the
kindness of the pleasant region, I saw drawing near the numerous chorus of a thronging crowd, festive
and dancing, of most delicate and splendid young men and youths, with unshorn hair exulting, with the
tresses of their ringed locks twisted without any pains, amorously crowned and combed with garlands and
little circlets of manifold flowers, and of vermilion roses, and of leafy myrtle, and of purple
amaranth mingled with melilot.

And with these an extreme multitude of most beautiful girls. More delicate and fair than would be found
in Sparta. And the one sex and the other clothed in a proud covering, not of Milesian wool, but rather
in richest garments of silk, and some of waving watered silk, not subject to the Oppian laws; some of a
changeable shot colour, belying the true colouring. Some of purple chosen from the murex, and some of
finest linen, such as is not produced in Egypt, white and crimped, and cloth crossed with saffron, most
subtly woven. And of countless other colourings. Some of sky-blue, others of crimson, many of green and
scarlet, of sandarac and of dark blue dyeing, with sumptuous delights; many of a saffron tincture, such
as Corycus does not bring forth, nor Centuripe, exceedingly gracious to the sight, threaded with a
golden filament. With a choice adornment of shining gems at the outermost hems about the ankles, held
by the purest gold. Some were mingled among them with sacred fillets and of a divine and pontifical
worship. Some with a hunting dress.
