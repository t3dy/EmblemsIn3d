In the lowest figure, on each square side, in the flat face, were carved Greek letters — three, one,
two, and three, in this order: DYS A LO TOS. In the round one I beheld three hieroglyphic characters,
perpendicular, beneath the feet of each image. And first was stamped the form of the Sun. Then, beneath
the next, an antique rudder. Last there appeared a dish, with a flame within it.

Above the outstretched corners of the dark stone, looking, I saw an Egyptian monster of gold, lying
four-footed. One of which had a wholly human face. The other half human and half beast. The third
wholly beastly. With a fillet going round the brow, with two ribbons covering the ears, hanging down,
and likewise licking along the neck and breast. And one descending down the back; with the body of a
lioness, the face turned forward.

Upon the back of each, then, there pressed a massive golden triangular pyramid, up to its topmost cover
five lines of the lowest face, or of its diameter. On each front there was carved a circle of that
measure, and above the circle a Greek letter, OMICRON. On the next face a circle, and above it a
letter, OMEGA. On the third flat a circle, and above it a letter, NY.
