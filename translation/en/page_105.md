Upon this projecting part sat a most noble vessel, and above this another of a different kind, but both
joined by two little handles, with an exquisite polishing and elegant workmanship and most precious
ornament. For among the other inestimable jewels, in its cover passing into a flower, out of which
there rose a diamond shaped into a little pear, with its point fixed in the flower, shining all over,
of an unheard-of and unimaginable size. And by my sense of smell I judged the water to be of roses,
mixed with the juice of lemon rind and a little amber, or benzoin, in a skilful gradation, with a
welcome and sweet scenting.

In the middle of this stupendous space there was set out a wonderful vessel for perfuming, not only for
its noble and perfect material, which was pure and best gold, but for its notable and antique making;
with its support upon three rapacious feet of a foul harpy. Which, in foliage, gave a binding to that
triangular base, plentifully adorned with little histories, as such a metal demands.

Upon the outstretched corners of which three naked spirits stood, set in order, two cubits high, with
their little shoulders turned one towards another, near one to another. They trod upon the base,
resting at the corner with the right foot, and with the other hanging and free, towards the fixed foot
of the next boy. And each of them, both elbows raised, held a shaft in the form of a pomegranate
flower, slender at the bottom, and in the upper part opening into a little shell for every hand,
widened at the mouth and a little hollowed, with broad rims. Six there were in a due circuit, cleaving
one to another.
