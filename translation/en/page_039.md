For which cause, greatly stirred by curious eagerness, I entered and climbed. Where I found the
greatest and most prodigious monster all hollow and void, and made a cavern — save that the same solid
mass was left within, which stood beneath as the ballast. And it had as much passage, both towards the
head and towards the hinder part, as a natural man could make his way through.

And there, hung in the vault of the back, there burned with brazen chains an inextinguishable lamp,
with a prison's lighting. By which, in this hinder part, I beheld an ancient sepulchre granted to the
stone itself, with a perfect image of a man, naked, of the common natural size, crowned, of the Stone,
most black. With the teeth, eyes and nails covered in shining silver. Standing above the arched
sepulchral cover, clothed in scale-work and other exquisite lineaments. It showed the forepart with a
gilded sceptre of bronze, the arm stretched out. And in the left hand it held a keel-shaped shield,
its form taken exactly from the skull-bone of a horse, inscribed in three tongues with little letters
— Hebrew, Attic and Latin — to this sense:

*I should be naked, had not the beast covered me. Seek, and you shall find. Let me be.*
