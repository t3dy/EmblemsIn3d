The whole space of the area, then, in the middle of which was raised this most notable artifice of the
famous and sumptuous fountain, had a stone pavement of squares of fine marbles of various colour and
shaping. In which, less than their measure, there were most beautifully fitted rounds of gracious
jasper, levelled with the utmost evenness, differing in their colouring. And the remaining corners were
beautifully satisfied with winding fronds and lilies.

Then the broad bands, or fillets, between the squares I beheld to be of the best tessellation of most
welcome coloured stones, with a minute cutting. In green leaves with crimson flowers, dark blue, purple
and grey-green, so much the better joined in a confederate way with an obstinate cohesion, the less I
should know how to express it — of an artful composition, and most bright with a choice shining, of
diligent polishing, a noble shaping. More lovely in colour than crystal of various tincture struck back
by the rays of the sun. Because the colours led about, with a most charming meeting, were reflected in
those burnished stones; no step betraying itself in the cut tesserae, lozenges, triangles and squares,
but with a most level directness, wholly equal.

By all which things I remained as it were dazzled in my senses and stupid, examining carefully within
myself the work, exceedingly notable, such as I was not used to seeing. And willingly I should have
wished to pause a little, and such dignity of workmanship would have needed a longer stay to
investigate, and to contemplate somewhat more diligently; but I could not, because it was fitting to
follow closely my eloquent companions and guides.

The aspect, then, of this sumptuous, magnificent and proud palace, and its approved situation or
placing, and the symmetry of its marvellous composition, at the first meeting won me to a chief
cheerfulness and comely grace; by the dignity of which making I was provoked to go on and contemplate
further. For which cause I deservedly judged that its most skilful builder was excellent above any
other that ever built. What framing, then, of beams and of rafters; what distributed disposition of
chambers and inner rooms and courts; what walls covered and crusted with precious panelling; what
