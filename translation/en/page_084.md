"Above my own life," I said, sighing. "Beyond all delights and above all the riches of whatever

charming girls bathed themselves with much solace, and I with them.

At the space opposite the notable fountain of the sleeping Nymph outside, within the bath there was
another of statues, artfully made of the best metal, with a golden and mirroring brightness. These were
fixed above a marble hollowed in a square and drawn forward into a frontispiece, with two little
half-columns — that is, in a half-circle, one to each side — with the little beam, the little frieze
and the little cornice carved in the solid of the single stone. This excellent composition offered
itself as the residue of the whole work, wonderfully accomplished with choice art and invention.

In the cut hollow, or in the emptied space of the said stone, two perfect Nymphs stood, a little less
than the natural size, unclothed as far as their thighs, where the parting of the shift they wore over
gave way, somewhat flying with the movement of their office; and their arms likewise bared, save from
the elbow to the shoulders. And above the arm that held up the little boy the garment was raised and
thrown back. The little feet of which infant — the one in the hand of the one Nymph, the other in the
other's hand — they pressed; the faces of all three laughing. And with the other hand the Nymphs,
moving aside the little boy's fringes as far as his girdle or navel, uncovered him. And the child held
his little member with both his hands. Which, within the warm waters, made water of the freshest,
cooling them.

In this delicious and most excellent place I was, by such conditions, wholly dissolved in gladness and
content; but the chief pleasure of my senses was interrupted, only because among these contented ones,
and among such whiteness and dew condensed into hoar-frost, I saw myself as it were Egyptian and of a
swarthy hue.

One of these, then, named Achoe, affably said to me, smiling: "Our Poliphilo, take that vessel of
crystal and bring me here a little of that fresh water." Without delay and without further weighing of
it — save that I was gratifying her, and showing myself not only promptly obedient but even eager to
please her — I went at once. And no sooner had I set one foot upon a step to get to the falling water
than the little water-maker lifted his spout and drew across my hot face the coldest water, so that
almost in that instant I fell back upon my knees. At which so shrill and womanish a laughter rang
beneath the blunt dome that I too began (having come to myself) to laugh so hard that I felt myself
dying of it.
