At the first table, afterwards, all having washed at the aforesaid fountain of skilful artifice — by
the force of gathered, or enclosed, air the water rose and was taken up again. Which thing being so
exquisite, I thought that by twin pipes, here and there unequal, through a wall set between, pierced in
the middle, the vessel was divided within, and by its own impulse the forced water ascended; which,
having learnt by a subtle examination, was extremely pleasing to me.

After the washing of all, to the Queen first and thereafter to each in turn, the beauty-loving serving
girls gave a golden apple, distinguishedly pierced through, and enclosing a paste of a wonderful
scenting — for this cause, that the idle hands of each should be occupied with some act by the said
ball, and the senses in looking and smelling; and it was adorned with precious little stones.

Here, then, at every change of dishes, two damsel stewards drew most beautifully into the middle of the
royal court a stupendous store-carriage upon four running wheels, in its forward part in the form of a
boat, or skiff, the hinder remainder shaped into a triumphal car of the purest gold, wrought fiercely
with manifold Scyllas and little water-monsters, and many most exquisite expressions, and wonderfully
finished, with richest little stones in an elegant distribution and wonderful ornament, filled
seemlily throughout — the sparkling of which shone through all the space that could receive it, and
meeting in a flashing with the glowing of the other jewels set variously on every side, you would
freely say that Phoebus was there with his shining locks, with a Nymph sitting, whose face made her no
less lovely with her shining eyes. So that, for the continual brightness and splendour of so many
unspeakable workmanships, no addition could be found, nor any equivalent comparison, were it even the
Babylonian temple with its three golden statues.

Within which were amply prepared all sauces and seasonings needful for the whole requirement of the
varied table; it carried cloths, flowers, cups, napkins, vessels, little forks, drink, food and
seasonings. This waggon Nymph then readily distributed the preparation to those who cleared away.
