Afterwards we shall all return together, made glad, to the great palace of our distinguished Queen.
Who, most clement in all things and most munificent of a wide liberality, will most ably suggest and
dispose your intense loves and burning desires and lofty conceits. Take heart and comfort. Let us go."

With pleasurable acts, with maidenly gestures, with persuasive semblances, with girlish caresses, with
wanton glances, with sweet little words, they led me there, solacing and coaxing. Content with
everything present — save that my golden-haired Polia was not there for the fulfilment of the highest
happiness, and to be a sixth with these and make up the perfect number.

On the other hand I found myself discontented, that my habit was not conformable amid this delicious
company; but having made myself somewhat at home, I began affably to dance with them. And they laughed
sweetly, and I likewise with them; and at last we came to the place.

There I beheld a wonderful eight-sided building of baths. And at every single outer angle two piers
were paired; level with the floor began the base-courses girt about beneath them. Then the piers
followed, a third of their thickness drawn from the solid, with capitals set beneath the straight
beam; with a frieze above, going entirely round beneath a cornice. Which frieze was adorned with
choice carving: with several naked boys, excellently expressed, equally distant, with their hands
entangled in nooses holding swelling little bundles of leafy twigs bound together, girt with thongs.

Above the said cornice there then rose (with an elegant vaulting) an eight-sided little Dome, in
imitation of what was beneath. Which, from angle to angle, was pierced through with a marvellous
working by way of a thousand noble conceits, and stopped with little plates of pure crystal, which
from afar I judged to be lead.

The little wing was fixed above a pointed thing, the eight-sided form of the Dome being sectioned,
somewhat eminent; and there, immediately set above, was a Triangle, in whose topmost centre, fixed,
there rose a most firm shaft, into which was let another shaft, unstable and turning, free and playing;
to which was fitted a vane, which, driven by whatever wind blew, turned the tubed shaft with it, and a
ball at the top of a third of the one below.

Upon this a naked boy sat, treading with his right heel, holding the other leg hanging. The back of
whose head, as far as the mouth, was all hollowed in the form of a funnel, with the orifice bored
through to the mouth; to which, soldered, was joined a trumpet, held with one hand by the boy near the
soldering, and the other stretched towards the end of the trumpet, levelled in a line with the vane.
