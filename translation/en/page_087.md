At this lamentable and mournful response of mine, straightway, their coral-red little mouths full of
laughing clamours strongly stirred, they came to such an excess that neither they nor I were able any
longer to walk for multiplying laughter. But falling down and rolling among the fragrant flowers and
upon the grassy ground, they were choked with unruly laughter; whence it was needful to slacken and
loosen a little the tight girdle across them. And lying thus half dead beneath the shady and leafy
trees, and in the spreading shade of the branches, they rested.

There, then, with a familiar boldness, I said to them: "O women full of fire, and mischievous to me, is
it thus you use me? See how a lawful occasion is offered me of breaking in and overwhelming you and of
doing you an excusable violence." And nodding towards them as though I would seize them, feigning a
boldness in doing what by no means I dared — they with fresh laughter called one another to help, and
fled, leaving their golden slippers and veils here and there, their fillets carried off by the cool
breezes; and their little vessels ran among the neglected flowers. And I running after. So much so that
truly I do not know that they did not swoon with it, and I as well, my powers laid low.

When, then, this comfortable make-game and this mocking sport had lasted a while, and my agitation thus
raised was fully satisfied, and the slippers and the other scattered things gathered up — near the
green and dewy banks of a running little stream, the sweet laughter being tempered and they most
tenderly pitying me, there by the trimmed banks of low and bending rushes and little willow, with the
osier swimming, and nearby plentiful in living water-simples, one of these, a well-mannered one called
Geusia, bending down, plucked up the Herculean water-lily, and a root of Arum, and Amella, which were
sprouting a little apart one from another; and laughing offered them to me, that I should choose which
of these pleased me and taste it for my deliverance. For which cause I refused the water-lily; I
condemned the little dragon-plant for its burning; I accepted the Amella. And this, being cleaned, she
persuaded me to taste. Whereupon it was no long space of time before, the slippery goad departing, the
