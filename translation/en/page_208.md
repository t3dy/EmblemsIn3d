in the greater lamp, and the greater in those, with inconstant splendour vacillating throughout all the
Sacred Temple. And by the mirror-sheen of the most-terse marbles — such an Iris the Sun, after the rain,
does not paint in the air. But above all, a marvellous thing this represented itself to the sight: for the
sculptor-artificer had perspicuously excavated, in circuit, upon the corpulence of the crystalline lamp, of
*cataglyph* (relief-hollowed), or coffered, work, a most-prompt battle of little infants riding upon the
knotty and swift Dolphins, with the tails inspiring themselves (curling), with manifold and dissimilar
effects and infantile efforts — not otherwise than if nature had feigned it; and they appeared not
excavated, but of raised work, and so-made expressed, that the intent of my eyes, away from so delectable an
object, from the accompanying Nymph, they violated (drew off).

## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** The **great crystal lamp** in full: a gourd-shaped inner crystal vessel holds the
wick, the sphere is filled with **five-times-distilled "burning water"** so the whole globe seems to blaze
like the sun (unbearable to look at); the four gem-lamps and the sphere reflect each other's coloured light
through the marble temple "like an Iris"; and the crystal is carved in relief with a **battle of putti
riding dolphins** that seems alive. All secure.

- **"Burning water, five times distilled."** *Aqua ardens* (distilled spirit/alcohol) reiterated five times
  through the still is the fuel: Colonna specifies rectified spirit, so the crystal sphere glows steady and
  blinding "as the Sun." A precise, period-accurate technical detail (early distillation), and a lighting
  cue — a brilliant central globe of white fire ringed by four coloured gem-lights.
- **the temple lit like a rainbow.** The five lamps' lights cross and reflect in the polished marble,
  filling the temple with shifting colour "such as the Sun does not paint after rain." The world's temple
  interior can be lit exactly so: one blazing white core, four jewel-colours, all thrown across mirror-stone.
- **the dolphin-riding putti** — the crystal globe is carved with a living-seeming relief of infants racing
  dolphins (a marine-love motif, fit for sea-born Venus), so vivid it pulls Poliphilo's eyes even from
  Polia. The lamp is both light and sculpture.
- **the flicker animates the carving** (p. 209) — the vacillating flame makes the dolphin-relief seem to
  move: Colonna notes the optical effect of firelight on carved crystal. A rendering idea (animated caustics).
