Afterwards, all being clothed — and they detained a good while longer in their nymphlike trimming,
combing and adorning — with great cheerful festivity they familiarly opened the vessels of the most
delicate confections, and comfortably tasted them, and I with them, the precious drink following after.
Then, sufficiently refreshed, and turning again to the mirrors with a scrupulous examination of the
adornment of their divine presences, and of the bright forehead shadowed with the little globes of
their hanging golden curls — and their damp hair wrapped in a clear covering — at last they said to me
joyfully:

"Poliphilo, let us go now with glad heart to our renowned and lofty Queen Eleuterylida, where you shall
feel a greater delight." And laughing they added: "Ha! the water did indeed strike you in the face."
And they renewed their sweet laughter without any measure, cheerfully making sport of me, nodding to
one another with a wanton blinking of the eyes and a sidelong, goatish look.

And thence, making a graceful departure and going in the midst of the festive girls, they began sweetly
to sing, rhythmically in the Phrygian mode, a droll metamorphosis. Namely, how a lover, wishing to
change himself by an ointment into a little bird, mistook the box, and was transformed into a rough
ass; concluding that some believe unguents to be for one effect, and afterwards they are for another.
By this I suspected that the point of it was aimed at me, from their looks turned mockingly towards me;
but at the time I thought nothing further of it.

Judging then, shrewdly, that that anointing had been for the relief of my weary limbs — behold, of a
sudden I began to be so stirred by a wanton itching and a goading desire, that I turned and twisted
wholly about. And those cunning ones laughed freely, knowing what had befallen me. So much did it
quicken that I felt myself every hour more sharply pricked. Whence I know not what bit, or what halter,
restrained me from falling upon them like a ravening and famished eagle that hurls itself out of the
air, rapacious and constant, upon a flock of partridges. Neither more nor less was I strongly goaded to
violence. And feeling so great an incitement grow every hour, it tormented me; and the more I burned,
bent beyond measure with desire, the more such fit and convenient objects offered themselves most
violently, stirred as I was by the increase of an almost deadly plague and of an unknown heat.
