These and like words Logistica said, stirred with vehemence and with indignant wrinkles rising on her
brow; and, throwing the lyre to the ground, she broke it. Whereupon Thelemia, quick and undaunted by
such urging, laughingly made me a sign that I should not attend to Logistica. For which cause
Logistica, knowing my wicked leaning, kindled with disdain, turned her back and, sighing, hurrying and
running, went out.

And I remained with my conquering and dear Thelemia, who, coaxing and cheerful, said to me: "This is
that place, Poliphilo, where before long you will find without fail the thing most loved by you, which
is yours, which is the thing of the world of which your obstinate heart thinks and longs without
ceasing." So that, scrupulously running it over within myself, I found only that nothing else was
stamped upon my wretched heart, thinkable and most desired, save my sun-born Polia. By these
