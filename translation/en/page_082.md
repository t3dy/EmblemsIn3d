The rest of the inner vault, apart from the stoppings of crystal, was all covered with a Cyanean
colouring of litharge, scattered and studded with gilded bosses, choicely shining.

Not far off there was in the earth a fissure, which continually vomited fiery matter; and taking of
this and stuffing the hollow of the vessel above, they laid on certain gums and scented woods, and made
an inestimable fumigation of a fragrance like the best perfumes. Then the twin leaves being closed,
pierced with metal and stopped with most limpid crystal, they gave a most joyful and manifoldly
coloured light. By this same piercing of open knots they clearly lit the fragrant baths, and the
fragrance was kept in, and the heat did not breathe out.

The levelled wall set between the one column and the other then showed a most black stone, of a
hardness rejecting metal, and shining. Enclosed by a surrounding band a quarter broad, of coral-like
jasper, adorned with a lineament of double gorges, or whorls. In the middle of such a wall, between the
one column and the other, resting there sat an elegant naked Nymph, with distinct resting and office,
of Gallatite stone of ivory brightness; firmly placed above upon fitting little altars, which, of round
lineament, composedly bound themselves in a circle with the bases of the columns. O how exquisitely
carved I beheld the said images, so that more than once my eyes allowed themselves to stray from the
true and real ones and to carry themselves to the feigned.

The paved little floor beneath the water was seen of various inlay of hard stones tessellated, in
marvellous drawings of divers colourings. Because the most limpid water — not sulphurous, but
sweet-smelling and temperately warm, without furnace or stoke-hole, and purged beyond all belief — was
no hindering medium between the object and the sense of sight. For the various little fishes, on the
fronts of the seats and of the floor, artificially expressed in mosaic in emulation of nature, appeared
