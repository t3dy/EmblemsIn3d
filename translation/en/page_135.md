# Page 135 — Chapter X

Above each of which, in Ionic, Roman, Hebrew and Arabic characters, I saw the title that the divine
Queen Eleuterylida had foretold and prophesied to me that I should find.

After we had come there, the damsel companions immediately began to interpret clearly and to make plain
the titles to be noted; and knocking at the resounding right-hand valves, closed, of metal, stained
with a greenish rust, without delay they were opened.

## Notes

**Confidence: low.** **The three doors' titles are missing and the page does not say so.** This is
the worst gap found in this stretch. The source transcription for this leaf is not text at all but a
Wikisource image-map stub (`File:Hypnerotomachia_Poliphili_pag135.jpg`, with `rect` co-ordinates and
the note *caratteri a caso!*), because the titles are cut in the woodcut and were never transcribed.
The English says only that Poliphilo "saw the title that the divine Queen Eleuterylida had foretold"
and prints none of the three, with no editorial note that anything is absent — so the reader cannot
tell that the three names on which the whole chapter turns have dropped out. They must be restored
from the facsimile before this page can be trusted, and until then a reader should treat the
door-scenes on pp. 136 to 138 as unlabelled.
