That little boy was exactly and artificially made of the white vein of the Agate, or Onyx; and the
winged one of the Sard, which is the other vein coming together with it. At which most exquisite
expression I stood stupefied, considering how the elegant craftsman so perspicaciously imagined to
apply that stone most aptly to such an office and purpose.

So that I reasonably conjectured, by the feathers somewhat ruffled about the beak, and the beak
half-open with the playing tongue showing, that she openly accused herself of being wholly intent and
dissolved in desire. With her back she imitated the curve of the keystone, and with it she likewise
carried the arching back of the little boy.

The remaining Arch-beam then, disposed in the soffit in squared coffers excellently lined, held
hanging within them the rosettes sharply finished — as much as was the compass of the Antae, from the
binding of the capitals; above which binding it stretched beyond the antae, beneath the beginning of
the bend of the vault of the entry, or opening, of the gate.

In the triangles which the arch made, there was a shrine-bearing figure in each, of a most noble
carving, of that artifice which the common sort call Cameo. With the garments imitating the maidenly
little body, flying and yielding in part to the beautiful calves, breast and upper arms, with the hair
loose and unshod, holding out towards the keystone the Trophy of victory. Which most smoothly took up
the whole triangular flat of most black stone, to show the truth of the metals, and the Nymphs milky
and most white. Behind the columning was seen the white panelling of the best marble, fitted on.
