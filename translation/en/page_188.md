But at once, dismayed, judging this deceitful, I rejected it as a false and light thought; and in an
instant, moved by a raging and roaring spirit, confounding myself, I said: Why do you waver,
Poliphilo? To die for love's sake is praiseworthy. And yet would it ever be, by my sad and malignant
ill-luck, that my sorrowful happening, and my heavy kindlings, and my noble love of such a Nymph should
be recited in the hollowed earth? And then that the fine and pliant reeds should sprout, which,
sounding, should afterwards make plain my growing and hurtful loves?

Not shutting out such wickedness of my wandering thoughts, I said rightly: Perhaps this woman, as she
shows, is a venerable goddess; and therefore talking Syrinx of Arcadia, in the reedy and marshy seats of
the river Ladon, would not have been given, shaken, to the goading and forward east winds, and to the
tumultuous and frost-bearing north wind, and to the blowing and cloud-bearing south, and to the
troubled and rain-bringing wind, if her untimely and unseemly speech in the presence of the goddesses
had been fitting. And answering Echo, by such a likeness, would not have been made into a last voice, if
she had spoken seemly.

And therefore, the gods being of themselves most pious, such contempt and negligent daring makes them
severe avengers. For which cause the companions of slow and searching Ulysses would deservedly have been
kept safe without the mortal peril of shipwreck, if they had not shamelessly and wickedly stolen the
fated herd of Apollo, watched over by the Nymphs Phaethusa and her sister Lampetie. And Orion likewise
would not have known the horrible vengeance, if he had not rashly set himself against cold and chaste
Diana. And the son of burning Phoebus was rashly struck by lightning from highest Olympus, and bound
eternally in the Stygian waves, for using the peony herbs.

If, then, I should show by any sign any unseemliness towards this divine Nymph, might not the like, and
worse, easily befall me? At last I came out of so great a stirring of my quarrelling mind.
