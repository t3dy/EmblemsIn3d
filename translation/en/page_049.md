Likewise also, in the left column-foot, a naked man of manly age was carved, kindly in aspect, in
which he showed the greatest swiftness. He too sat upon a squared seat, adorned with most ancient

Where also that same Matron, naked, figured as a goddess — in whose narrow breast two little paps
budded, unmoved in their hardness and their roundness, with ample flanks, so conformably expressed with
the other that, sealed, they counterfeited one and the same form — offered that same boy, her son, to
this teaching man. Who, the little boy being already beside him, standing before him upon his little
feet, bent himself down and shrewdly showed him three arrows. By which act one easily conjectured that
he taught him by what art he should use them in his working. And the divine mother held the quiver
empty, and the bow likewise unstrung. At the feet of this master lay a viperous caduceus.

Here likewise was found the armed man, and a helmeted woman, who bore upon a spear a Trophy of a most
ancient breastplate hung up, and at the top a sphere with two wings; and between the one wing and the
other stood written thus: NOTHING IS FIRM. Clothed in a flying shift, with a showing of her breast
above.

The two ready Doric columns of Porphyry, of seven diameters, pressed upon each of these squares thus
explained; of a dark crimson colour with their little roundels lighter, confusedly scattered, shining
and polished. Channelled, with twenty-four Flutes apiece, between the most exact little cords. But of
the three parts, the lowest one was cabled.

The reason why they were so carved, hollowed and with the third cabled, I judged to be this: that this
most excellent fabric, or temple, must have been ritually dedicated to the one sex and the other —
that is, to a God and a Goddess; or to a mother and a son; or to a father and a wife; or to a father
and a daughter, and the like. And therefore the skilled ancient fathers attributed to the female sex
the greater part of hollowing, and to the male the cabling, because that slippery nature exceeds the
manly in wantonness.

The cause of all the fluted ones was for the temple of a Goddess, the flutes showing the wrinkled
