Here, in fitting order and distance, was a way set with cypresses, straight and lofty, with their
angled and cracked cones, as thick with foliage as by their nature they could be, and composedly
placed. And the levelled ground covered on every side with the greenest periwinkle, abounding in its
little azure flowers. Which adorned way, broad with a due spaciousness, tended straight towards a green
enclosure; and to the opening of that the cypresses were distributed by the line, for a length of four
stadia.

Coming joyfully to that cloister, I found it equal-sided, of three walls, in the likeness of a straight
wall, as high as the lofty cypresses of the way. Which was all of choice citrons, oranges and lemons,
thickly heaped together with a most welcome foliage and knit with an artful cohesion; and I judged its
thickness to be six feet. With a door bent in the middle out of the tree-work itself, composedly
carried through with the diligent industry of the maker, as well as one could say or make it. Above,
in a fitting place, windows were set in order. So that on the surface no wood or stem whatever showed
itself, but only the pleasant and welcome green of the flowering fronds.

Among the fair, thick and living leaves it was most abundantly adorned with white blossom, breathing a
sweet orange scent; and to the desirous eyes ripe fruits and unripe offered themselves plentifully, in
the highest degree delectable. Then within the thickness I looked at the branches (not without wonder),
so compacted by such mastery that by them one climbed easily through the whole capacious composition.
Whence, by the propping of the knitted branches, those who climbed did not appear.

Entering, then, into this green and most welcome enclosure — to the eyes exceedingly worth looking at,
and to the understanding worthy of esteem — I saw that it was an elegant cloister facing a wonderful
and most ample palace, excellent in its symmetried architecture and very magnificent. Which rendered
the fourth wall of the leafy enclosure, of a length of sixty paces. And this circuit was a square open
court beneath the sky.

In the middle part of this most notable area I saw an excellent fountain of the clearest waters,
spouting on high almost to the top of the green enclosure through the narrowest little pipes, and
falling down into a wide basin, which was of the finest amethyst, its diameter containing three paces,
of a thickness of a quarter, dwindling towards the lips to an inch, of most excellent casting; and all
round about there appeared, in relief, most worthily expressed, water-monsters — as many as the
