In which cleanest waters there gazed ardently at himself the purple and flowering son of the Nymph
Liriope, out of his tender leaves; and the river-loving and crimson water-mint; and here and there the

among the greenest and most joyful grass.

Which blessed place was of an ample and widest circuit, ringed with shrubby little mountains of a
moderate height, plentiful with green laurel, with fruit-bearing arbutus, and with tufted and loftiest
pines and firs, with white and temperate myrtle; and round about the clearest little channels with a
gravelly and sandy bed, and in some places the ground was of tawny sand; there lived the watery and
three-leaved dryope, in which the sloping water flowed with a soft murmur.

Here, then, was a great plenty of delicate and divine Nymphs, soft in years, with the fragrant flower of
modesty, beautiful beyond belief, with their beardless lovers, perpetual dwellers and citizens of this
most worthy place.

Of which Nymphs some, comelily, with their forward faces solacing themselves in the brightest waters,
had gathered up most beautifully their finest garments of silk, shining with a various and welcome
tincture, and, those being gathered at their snowy arms, they showed the elegant form of their full
flanks beneath the living little folds. And their whitest legs revealed, and their round calves shown
as far as their fleshy knees. And the running purest waters bathed them near their round ankles.

Which then, reflecting the excessive whiteness of their bright and shining limbs, and their neat
faces, among the little waves not leaping up, and their heavenly faces — as in a shining and most
burnished mirror likewise, where the current was not great — the waters, feigning, showed them; and
their little feet broke the met waters, and the opposing ripples, with an oncoming bursting and a
sounding meeting, invading them.

Some, solacing themselves, ran girt through the waters with the swimming and tame web-footed swans. And

out of the flowing streams, standing upon the soft herbs, busily wove jewels of the fragrant and
