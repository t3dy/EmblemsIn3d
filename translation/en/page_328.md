# Page 328 — Chapter XXII

Next in sequence, one raised a trophy with special polish. On a point passed off the
topmost spear-shaft; descending below it, a covering over a rounding half a sextant
thick, resupine in the manner of a plate, in the middle of which was carved a circled
formula. Which a small vase-stalk suppressed. Then a tablet with this majuscule writing
subjacent:

> **QUIS EVADET?**
> — "Who shall escape?"

To this an apple was set below, and next another rounding, like the upper but smaller.
Walled round by a rim, and superimposed upon a solid, or massy, little dish. From which,
continued, descended a longish baluster, and then a little basin.

Likewise another Nymph carried a spear. On the pointed end was an egg-shaped figure, with
a bossed little rim in a circle, and in the middle a sapphire rounding, imitating the
figure, an inch thick; set under it, a small board, thus with the title:

> **NEMO**
> — "No one."

And between two wings the spear, somewhat balustered, passed through. At the bottom a
little dish followed, as is recounted above.

## Notes

**Confidence: high** (the two inscriptions are read straight from the woodcut; the trophy-
description is secure). Two emblematic trophies bearing the paired mottoes **QUIS EVADET?**
("Who shall escape?") and **NEMO** ("No one") — one of the most famous devices in the whole
book, and one Colonna clearly means to be read as a single sentence.

- **"QUIS EVADET? — NEMO."** Read together across the two trophies: *Who escapes Love? No
  one.* The question and its answer are split between two spears carried in sequence, so the
  procession itself utters the sentence as it passes. This is the emblematic heart of the
  triumph — Love's universal dominion stated as a call-and-response.
- **The device's afterlife.** The *QUIS EVADET* trophy — a candle/vessel guarded by the
  motto — became one of the most-copied images from the 1499 woodcuts. Here its original
  context is restored: it is a trophy in Cupid's triumph, paired with **NEMO**.
- **the sapphire "imitating the figure"** — an egg-shaped sapphire an inch thick set as the
  centre of the NEMO trophy: Colonna's habitual precise gemmology, even on a parade-standard.
- Together with pp. 326–327, five trophies now precede the car; the triumphal vehicle itself
  is described in the pages following.
