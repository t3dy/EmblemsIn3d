## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** The fountain's dedication, the bawdy joke, and the water-herbs. Short, and
the main flag is editorial: the 1499 carves ΠΑΝΤΩΝ ΤΟΚΑΔΙ in Greek capitals and the English prints
only "To the Mother of all things", dropping the inscription itself — the third such substitution in
a dozen pages (see pp. 69 and 75). The joke is handled by euphemism to the point of concealment: *il
bellissimo suscitabulo dello instrumento* becomes "the most beautiful awakening of the instrument",
which no English reader will decode, though the punchline that follows — the coldness telling him
the stone was lying — does survive. *Vaticinio* as vervain is a plausible but unconfirmed
identification.

*Confidence stated 2026-09-20 by a second reader, not by the translator (ticket tr-confidence-unstated-180).*
