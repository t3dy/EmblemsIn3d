Beneath which Half-round figure I saw this word inscribed: TIME.

Then I saw besides, on the other side, many striplings — the work of the aforesaid craftsman,
altogether perfect, in a figure uniform with the one just recited, most beautifully waved, and the
waving of both figures clothed with exquisite foliage — intent upon gathering flowers among many
herbs and shrubs; while together many merry Nymphs, jesting playfully, coaxingly snatched them away.
And in the manner recited above, beneath the figure were certain hollowed Capitals which said this
one word: LOSS. And they were choice letters, exactly made, their thickness a ninth part, and a
little more, of the diameter of the square.
