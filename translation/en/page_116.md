From the bottom of this there began a flowery tail of a dolphin, joined somewhat to the slenderness of
the vessel set beneath, and it descended with the head finned with fronds above the surrounding girdle,
where the boyish heads were fixed. And with a moderate hump, or bending, near the head and curving
towards the tail, it made most elegant handles. And all this sloping part showed, with an exquisite
polish, the expression of the best lineaments.

Such a topmost vessel, so placed, was so perfectly wrought that when the two-wheeled carriage was
moved, the shaft with the vessel joined to it turned round, spinning and pouring out the water from
within the tree; and when the wheels stood still, the turning ceased. And in this way I thought that
its spinning had its force from one of the wheels, which held another toothed one towards the turning
spindle, which had the sockets for the teeth, and moved the stem of the vessel. The wheels were half
covered by a winged work; there appeared, as it were, two spread wings, one here and the other there,
adorned with certain Scyllas.

This marvellous work, running before each of us, our hands being moistened and then our faces, all made
fragrant with an unthinkable scent, we rubbed our hands, so that never such nor so great a scent
offered itself to my senses. And in this manner the most apt girls bestowed it.

Bedewed, then, with such a sprinkling of the most fragrant water, the mistress's servers, with a
singular showing of kindness, offered a golden goblet, with which first the sovereign Queen, having
with a chief affability greeted us all, drank the sweetest nectar; and thereafter in order, one with
another, with reverent and gracious and mutual honourings, we solemnly drank. An excellent closing and
sealing of all the favours received and of the sweet tasting.

Last of all the sweet-smelling flowers were diligently swept up, and all gathered and all the leavings
carried thence away, the paving remaining bright and shining as the brightness of the most burnished
mirror, rivalling the aspects standing round about and the shining gems; and, each sitting in the place
appointed, the Nymph of the fountain departed.

Last of all the magnanimous and lofty Queen at once ordered an unheard-of dance, or ball, upon the
jasper squares, finished with an exact artifice, burnished and bright — such a thing as among mortals
was never seen nor devised.
