Without delay, after the most welcome draught, this splendid table was taken down. And, the fragrant
flowers being scattered upon the shining pavement, a cloth was readily stretched out of purple silk
cloth; and they strewed over it flesh-coloured, or mallow-pink, roses, white, vermilion, musk,
damascene, four-petalled and Ghiebbedine, mingled.

And promptly the nine servers, clothed in the said cloth and colouring, set down before each of us six
cuttings of a blindly fattened head, moistened and sprinkled with its own poured fat and saffroned
rose-water, and mixed with orange juice, excellently roasted and then all gilded over, and with six
fitting slices of snowy Mnestorian bread. And thereafter with a seasoning of lemon juice tempered with
fine sugar, with pine seeds and with its liver ground, adding rose-water, musk and saffron and choice
cinnamon; and thus all the seasonings composed with a chief and exquisite gradation, shared out and
excellently seasoned. All the vessels were of topaz, and the round table.

This third frugal and magnificent table being, as above, unprepared, without delay the next was renewed
and covered with a silken cloth of satin of a bright yellow colour (and the servers clothed in the
same), and all flowered with fragrant flowers of lilies of the valley and of narcissus. Immediately
such a dish was set out: seven balls of partridge flesh with a sharp diligence, in broth, toasted, and
as many mouthfuls of eyed and milky bread. Seasoning: maple-sap, almonds ground with thrice-boiled
sugar, starch, citron sandalwood, musk and rose-water. Vessels of chrysolite, and the circular table.
Last they offered the most precious cup, and thus it was observed in those that followed.
