And in this ordered manner there were continually heard most welcome sounds, most charming harmonies
were listened to, delightful melody was felt, a most joyful scenting was drawn in, and a most splendid
fullness was received, tasting most sweetly. Everything, then, agreed mutually in dignity, in grace and
in delight, without any defect.

At this first and splendid table all the table utensils, or instruments, were of the finest gold, and
of this the round table before the Queen. And there was presented a cordial confection: as far as I was
able to conjecture accurately, it was a helpful and most excellent composition of shaved horn — that
is, of unicorn — with the two sandalwoods; pearls ground in burning water kindled at the fire, and
quenched in it down to the last cutting; manna, pine kernels and rose-water, musk, ground gold, very
preciously composed and weighed, and pressed into morsels with the finest sugar and starch. Of this she
gave two takings, at a moderate interval, and without drink, to each — a food to forbid every obstinate
fever, and to shut out any sad weariness.

This done, in an instant all things were taken up and removed, and the fragrant violets scattered on
the ground, and the table stripped bare. And no sooner was this so done than once again the table was
covered with sea-purple cloth, and likewise all the servers; and as at the first they covered it with
flowers of citron, orange and most fragrant lemon.

And here they presented, in vessels of beryl — and of this gem was the royal table (except the little
forks, which were of gold) — five little cakes, or fritters, of a saffron paste kneaded with hot
rose-water and sugar, and again sprinkled with a shower of the said finest musked water, frosted over
with the finest sugar. These little globes, of so sweet and varied a tasting, were diligently cooked
with this distinction: the first offering in oil of orange flowers; the second in oil of cloves; the
third in oil of jasmine flowers; the fourth in oil of the finest benzoin; the last in oil pressed of
musk and amber. Having tasted this delectable dish savourily and with an eager and greedy appetite,
