The remainder of the little dragons crept along the moderate hollowing of the basin, until they all
came together, their tail-ends transformed into antique foliage and into a most pleasant binding with
the little altar set beneath the three images, or rather their footstool, at a proportioned height.
Without any deforming encroachment upon the hollow of the precious basin.

So that, by the green meeting of the orange cloister, and by the shining of the bright material, and by
the purest waters, there was rendered a most welcome colouring, like a rainbow among hollowed clouds,
within the noble, proud and elegant vessel.

Then, in the swelling body of the basin, between the one little dragon and the other at an even space,
there stood out of that excellent casting heads of maned lions, with exquisite exactness, which,
vomiting, scattered through a devouring pipe the water dripping from the six little conduits set in the
most beautiful horns of plenty. Which water rose with so bridled a force that its fall came down among
the little dragons into the wide and sounding basin, with a most welcome ringing of the wide-open
vessel from the high fall of the said waters.

Of this most rare workmanship, then, chiefly built with so sharp a wit — such as was that uncommon
vessel, and the four most perfect harpies, and of what choice dignity was the little altar, where I saw
the three figures of most shining gold sitting, and with what artifice and polish it was ordered — I
should never know how to speak briefly and clearly, and still less am I fit to describe the whole. A
making not of human wit. But I may lawfully testify (calling the gods to witness) that never in our own
age was such a chased work, nor anything near its equal, devised more welcome or more worth beholding.
