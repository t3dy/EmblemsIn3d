There comes besides the dignity of the windows and of the notable door, and of the most noble podium,
the distinguished expression of the builder's art. No less excellent to see was the marvellous ceiling,
most beautifully coffered, with little coffers between the wavings, covered with foliage, set in square
and round. Adorned with exquisite lineaments, gilded of pure gold and of a dark blue colouring, and
elegantly painted. Let every other wonderful building be lost here.

Being now come to the opening of the notable door — behold, the way in was closed by a joyful and
marvellous curtain stretched across, all of golden thread, and rewoven and inwoven with silk, with two
most worthy images. The one of them was surrounded with every instrument fit for working; and one, with
her maidenly face raised, considered the heavens intently. The beauty of which not undeservedly warned
me off from thinking that any pencil (even the famous Apelles's) could reach it.

Here the eloquent and most beautiful and charming companions each joined her right hand kindly with
mine, wishing to lead me in, and receiving me they said: "Poliphilo, this is the order to be kept, by
which one must enter into the venerable presence and lofty majesty of our Queen. This chief and first
curtain it is granted to no one to enter without the receiving of a plain and watchful damsel
doorkeeper, called Cynosia." And she, hearing our coming, at once presented herself and courteously
drew aside the curtain. And we entered.

Here was an enclosed space, divided by another veiling of most noble artifice and composition, and
varied with every tincture. In which were signs, shapes, plants and animals of a singular reweaving. In
this place, at our coming, a woman likewise careful came immediately to us, named Indalomena. And,
frankly unfastening her curtain, we were led in.
