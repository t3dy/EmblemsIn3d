# Page 168 — Chapter XIV

In the other tablet was stamped a noble young man. Who with the utmost religion received the protection
of a crystal shield; and he, valiant, with the curved and cutting falchion beheaded a terrifying woman,
and proudly carried the severed head in sign of victory. From whose blood there was born a winged
horse, which, flying to the peak of a mountain, made a mysterious fountain rise with its hoof.

On the front face there was seen the powerful Cupid, who, shooting with his golden arrow towards the
starry heavens, made drops of gold rain down amorously. And an endless crowd of every condition,
wounded, stood all astonished at it. Opposite, I saw Venus in anger, having with an armed man loosed
her son, whom she had caught by the wings in a fated net, taking her revenge,
