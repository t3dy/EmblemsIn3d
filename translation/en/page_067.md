And so, with my breast continually beating and with a long-suffering mind, I said within myself: what
now persuades me to turn back? Is not fleeing and free escape easier here? And much better, I think, to
expose my doubted life in this light beneath the open sky, than cruelly to perish in the blind
darkness? Nor indeed should I know how to return to that opening and way out.

And in a moment, drawing from the depth of my sad heart groaning sighs, I turned over in my tenacious
remembrance how much pleasure and delight my senses had lost in an instant; for that working was full
of marvel and of stupefaction. Thinking again in what manner I was so ill deprived of it. Imagining to
myself the bronze Lions of the temple of that most wise Jew, which by frightening brought men into
oblivion.

Therefore, by such a likeness, I almost doubted that the dragon had done the same to me: that so many
elegant and marvellous makings and stupendous conceits, showing themselves not human, most worthy of
the telling, which I had diligently beheld, I must now let lightly flee from my dried memory; and that
by such an event I should not know how to narrate them in order.

I said: certainly this is not so, nor do I feel in myself any lethargic affliction. But I hold it all
placed most freshly in my understanding and my memory, kept safe, and painted indelibly. And truly that
huge beast was alive and not feigned, and so fearful, rarely seen such by mortals — alas, such as
Regulus did not see. And remembering it, my fallen hair rose again, and I hastened my nimble step.

Then, returning to myself in a moment, I said: here without doubt (as I shrewdly judge from the
kindness of the present site) there must dwell none but human folk. But rather perhaps divine spirits
and heroes are guardians here, and a lodging of Nymphs and of the ancient Gods. Therefore persuasive
appetite easily provoked my reined-in step, exhorting me to the journey begun. Whereupon I, as though
captured by persevering goads, with a fierce mind resolved to follow wherever playful fortune might
fall, though still wasting away.

Considering therefore that beautiful and pleasant country, and the fruitful fields and fertile
plains, and conjecturing the delight of them, I praised such an invitation most highly; and casting
behind me every trembling restraint of mournful fear, I entered somewhat. But first I invoked the
divine light, and the prospering Genii, that they should be present to guide me at this my entering,
and be Companions to my wandering Pilgrimage, and bestowers of their holy guidance.
