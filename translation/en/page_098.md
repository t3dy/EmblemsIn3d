All which most excellent things, of an incomparable, incredible and almost unthinkable cost, flashed
everywhere most precious, not only for the most noble and marvellous material, but likewise for the
great and most exquisite making. Weighing which marvellous thing with a most subtle examination, I
pondered upon the wandering extension of those most intricate branches, and proportioned in thickness.

So artful — by what art, and what rash daring, and what obstinate intent were they so aptly carried
through: whether by the workman's glue, or by soldering, or by hammering, or by the founder's art? By
these three conditions of working and forging metal it seemed to me impossible that a covering of such
a breadth and knitting should be so excellently made.
