In the presence of the most magnificent Queen there always waited three reverent and neat girls, with
the trimness of veiling garments of gold and silk woven in a wonderful manner. The colour graciously
changing to the eye, of the colouring of the table-cloths, so that as the cloths were changed, in the
same manner the nymphlike garments of the servers were varied as to colour; with a most charming
gathering of the drapery beneath their tight girdle, turning from their fleshy and snowy shoulders, and
drawn over the full breast moderately swelling. Shod with golden shoes with a moon-shaped opening over
the bare foot, all alike fastened with golden clasps. With flowing hair, blond and most abundant, and
falling to their calves. On the whitest forehead girt with little fillets of great and uniform pearls.

These three stood before her with a singular and devout reverence, very heedful and set to such an
office with a chief and ready service; who served at one table only. When afterwards the next change of
table came, all these stood upon their feet, made servants with their arms folded in the utmost
reverence. And thus in succession all the others observed it, and always as many again were renewed in
number.

Of the three servers to each guest: the one between the two offered the food; she on the right side
accompanied that food underneath with a little plate, so that nothing should fall elsewhere. The third,
on the left, elegantly wiped the lips with a most white napkin, fine and cleanest. At every act the
reverence was ready. The napkin was never used a second time for that office, but, thrown by the damsel
upon the pavement, was at once gathered up by those standing by and carried thence away. And as many
mouthfuls as were to be offered, so many scented and perfumed folded napkins they brought with them for
changing, silken, of wonderful woven workmanship.
