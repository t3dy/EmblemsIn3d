And with such and like lineaments the frieze extended itself in its adornment. Which a most seemly
cornice covered, composed of every polish of artifice. Above which, on the flat, perpendicular to the
projection set over it in the order of the squares, there were vessels shaped in the most ancient
manner, set at an ordered distance, a good three feet high and more, of chalcedony, some of agate, some
of a vermilion amethyst, and some of garnet and of jasper, alternated in colour, of various and notable
workmanship most subtly chased, their bodies chiefly adorned with little hollows twisted and straight.
And with masterly and excellent handles.

To the order and line of each jewel above the cornice there were aptly fixed square little beams seven
feet high, of shining gold, hollow within, with a further stretch of like little beams above them going
round the straight ones. And traversed crosswise in trellis-work with a ruled partition, they came
together seemly. Understanding that out of the vessels set in the corners of the walls the little beam
and the vine came out together. But out of the remaining vessels there rose alternately either a vine
