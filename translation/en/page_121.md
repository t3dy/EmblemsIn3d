In the third dance, all being ordered and set out in their places, the musicians tightened the measure
of the time still further, with the manner and tone of the stirring Phrygian — such a sounding as
Marsyas of Phrygia never knew how to find. The King clothed in gold made her that stood before the
Queen move upon the third square, going straight in her first passage.

For which cause there was at once seen a battle, a tournament, so delightful, with so ready and sudden
a vehemence; with bending down to the very ground, and then making a swift and twisting leap, and like
a turning tumbler, with two rollings in the air, the one opposite the other. And then without delay,
the right foot set to the ground, she turned round three times. And then at once with the other foot
she went round the contrary way; all this action they used up in one measure, so fittingly and with so
great an agility that nothing could go beyond it: with their deep bowings and ordered spinnings and
easy leaping, with comely gestures, as much as ever of such and like a thing was seen, nor ever could
be beheld, nor was ever attempted.

Nor was the one ever a hindrance to the other; but whoever was taken by the taker, the honeyed kiss
being given her in an instant, went out of the game; and the fewer that remained, the more one saw a
most charming skill in the deceiving of one by another. Such a worthy order and manner was kept by each
without defect, however briefly the measuring of the learned and excellent musicians hastened it — and
it stirred no less all those standing by to such movements, by the agreement of the consonant harmony
with the soul, being here chiefly and above all in the highest and concordant consent of the well-being
of their disposed bodies.

For this reason, of the power of Timotheus that most skilful musician, I warmly thought that he with
his song forced the army of the great Macedonian to take up arms again, and then, turning back the
voice and the tone, to leave their arms neglected and provoke them all to ceasing. Of this third game
she that was clothed in gold in royal form gloriously triumphed.

This most joyful festival being famously ended with an extreme gladness and the greatest solace, all
set themselves to sit. And here, being made to rise, and before the venerable seat of her divine
majesty I made a deep reverence, and seemly kneeling, she spoke to me thus: "Poliphilo, put now into
forgetfulness the past and befallen cases, and thence the wearisome thoughts, and the danger gone by,
for I am certain that at present you are fully restored. Since, then, you wish to go on undaunted in
the amorous flames of Polia, I judge it a fitting thing that for this recovery you should go to the
