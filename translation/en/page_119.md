But since I found myself thus, and in so great a heaping of glory, and a holy site, and a happy
country, and a blessed delight, and at the frugal and triumphing banquet — such as Clodius the
tragedian never made, subject neither to the Tapullan nor the Licinian law — refreshed with a moderate
fullness, and none the less by the royal promise of favouring my loving desire not vainly made secure,
I comforted myself, holding as nothing all that had until now befallen me and been set in my way; and
of everything I spoke well of Fortune, with a joyful gladness.

For which cause, the lofty Queen wishing to show, for a greater display beyond the things aforesaid,
the excess and the surpassing of the universe in all excellent and rarest magnificences — everyone
sitting in their places, after the miracle of the sumptuous banqueting, without any long delay she
ordered a game to be looked at, worthy not so much of the sight as of an eternal remembrance, which
also was an excellent dance, or ball, in such a course and manner.

Through the way of the curtains there entered thirty-two young girls, of whom sixteen were clothed in
cloth of gold (but eight of one uniform). Then one of those sixteen clothed in gold was dressed in
royal habit, and another in the dress of a Queen, with two keepers of the rock, or citadel; two silent
ones, or secretaries; and two knights. With an equal number, eight were clothed in cloth of silver, with
the same offices.

All these, according to their office, disposed themselves thus, placing themselves upon the squares of
the pavement: that is, sixteen clothed in gold on the one side, and sixteen in silver on the other,
opposite.

The musicians began to play with three instruments of daring invention, very concordant and sharing
together, with a most sweet consonance and tuned melody. At the measured time of the sound, upon their
squares, as the King commanded, the dancing and dolphin-like tumblers moved, and with the most seemly
turnings, honouring the King and the Queen, they climbed upon the next square, making an excellent
pause.
