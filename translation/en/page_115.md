## Notes

**Confidence: medium.** A short page: the topaz vessel on the turning shaft, with its four winged
boys' heads and their dripping pipes. Accurately rendered, and the proportion (the upper part
narrowing to twice the lower body) is transcribed as the Italian gives it. Medium because the page
is nothing but vessel-anatomy in standard equivalents, because it breaks off mid-sentence at both
ends, and because *scindule* is given as "slices", which is literal but leaves the reader guessing
at the gadrooning it describes.

*Confidence stated 2026-09-20 by a second reader, not by the translator (ticket tr-confidence-unstated-180).*
