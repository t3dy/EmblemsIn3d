This solemn change being carried away, without any interval a cloth of violet silk was spread, and the
nymphlike garments likewise. For flowers, the three kinds of jasmine, crimson, yellow and white. For
food, nine mouthfuls from the breast of the ever-keeping peacock, moist in its broth and roasted in its
fat. A sauce greenest and somewhat sharp, with pistachios ground, Cyprian sugar, starch, musk, thyme,
wild thyme, white oregano, and pepper. Vessels of dark blue sapphire, and the royal table.

At this seventh sumptuous change they carried away the sumptuous board, all of the whitest ivory subtly
inlaid, and brought back another of precious aloe wood, excellently compacted with glue, and from one
end to the other hollowed with a wonderful devising into most noble knot-works of foliage, flowers,
vessels, little monsters and small birds, and filled in with a black paste of musk and amber in a
confederate mixture. This I rightly judged a most elegant and sumptuous thing, breathing a delightful
scent; the cloth whitest and fine, with a fine-wrought weaving of byssus of Carystus, and of such cloth
the adorned garments of the serving girls. Flowers of cyclamen, with all manners of gillyflowers
exceedingly fragrant. So that whoever might be able to consider so sweet and various a fragrance, with
its continual and new meeting most joyful to the sense, I do not dare to express.

An excellent confection in morsels: pulp of dates with pistachios, ground with rose-water, and island
sugar, musk, adulterated with precious ground gold, so much that all the takings appeared of gold;
three to each were given. The vessels of jacinth, and the circular table — a stone fitting to so
excellent an ordering of the divine board and its splendour, not subject to the Licinian law.

After the joyful and most welcome tasting of this marvellous confection, and the flowers rolled to the
ground, by a royal magnificence there was brought without delay a great golden basin full of kindled
embers, into which the cloth was thrown and the napkins of byssus; and so long a space they left them
in the fire that all was kindled and glowing, and thence drawn out and cooled, it was seen unhurt,
purified and cleanest, as before. And this too was a distinguished and unheard-of showing. Last of all
the tripods and the boards were readily taken down and carried thence away.
