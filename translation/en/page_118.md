Receiving-rooms capacious and wonderful in their art and incredible cost, with the choice praise of
their most distinguished maker, every partition and elegant agreement of the distinguished lineaments
deservedly approved. I beheld afterwards, with a singular pleasure, a coffered ceiling in the highest
degree skilfully adorned, stretched in an even flat, without peer or comparison; it made a proud
heaven, with set spacings of manifold shaping, with a drawn and levelled measure, hedging in the little
areas with a noble composition, with a ready prominence, which was duly adorned with the lineament of a
cornice, with little bands, gorges and egg-work, berries, or fruits of roses evenly threaded. And
leaves of acanthus licking along the corners of the square and oblong little areas, with exact roses
with a twinned order of foliage, the inner one smaller, with the required interval, all open, and the
curved parts, in a greater relief, everything clothed, or gilded, with the best and shining gold, and
with the finest and choicest dark blue colouring. With various other figurings of the ornament matching
the lineament — let the beamed roof of Salauces, king of the Colchians, yield here.

Then the voluptuous pleasantness of the ordered green places and orchards, and watered gardens, living
fountains, with little streams running in marble enclosures, held and hedged in with an incredible
making. Grass ever fresh with dew, and flower-bearing, and sweet summer airs and spring winds with the
various harmony of small birds. The pure serenity and unfailing temper of the sky, and the wholesomeness
of a freest and purest air; places neither low nor stony, nor swept by constant and frosty winds, nor
burnt by the unruly and scorching sun, but lit with an easy and kindly weather, and sincerely most
joyful with moderation and a middle measure; the fields fertile and untilled yet fruitful of every
good, sunny hills, leafy and cool little woods, pleasant with thick shade.

Afterwards the inestimable furnishing, the ready and manifold household and elegant service, the varied
youth with its growing age. The most welcome presence of the girls of the hall, of the court, of the
chamber, and of the royal household; the venerable and majestic sight of them, with a dress more lovely
and seemly and a distinguished ornament, and with an approved and most charming comeliness, as much as
ever anyone could feel or tell.
