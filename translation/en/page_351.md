# Page 351 — Chapter XXII

The Area contained, by diameter, 32 paces. The thickness of the composite (the built ring), 8 paces.

The partition of the encompassing colonnade was quadrifariously divided over the circumference. Between
one and the other interjected partition stood, with even discrimination, eight divisions, where the
columns were constituted and set. From which the solid proceeded toward the centre; and, both by the
straight and by circuit, transverse in a ring, stood commensurately the columnar supports; between one
and another, partitely, the gaps of the openings, corresponding linearly, passable, and inter-void, and
artfully vaulted the porticoes, or fornices — the lines narrowing with wondrous convenience, the straight
and the circling transverse, the altitude reserved, and every rule accessory to the harmony. The
pavement floor was egregiously sectile, of marvellous invention and of conspicuous art —

## Notes

**Confidence: high** (the dimensions are plain figures); medium for the vaulting description. **The exact
plan of the theatre**: the central Area is **32 paces in diameter**, the built ring **8 paces thick**; the
surrounding colonnade is divided into four quarters, each of eight bays, with radial and concentric columns
carrying vaulted porticoes. All secure, and directly load-bearing for the world's reconstruction.

- **The theatre's dimensions close the island's arithmetic.** Area diameter = **32 paces**; this is exactly
  twice p. 325's "theatre, from its middle to the centre, 16 paces" — the two measurements agree. The built
  ring around it is 8 paces thick. The world's central theatre should be a 32-pace-diameter obsidian floor
  (see p. 352) ringed by an 8-pace-deep vaulted arcade.
- **the colonnade's plan: 4 × 8 bays.** The encompassing colonnade is quartered, each quarter of eight bays,
  columns set both radially and concentrically to carry a ring of vaults — a precise, buildable ambulatory
  plan. The "lines narrowing toward the centre" are the same radial geometry as the terraces (p. 315).
- **sectile pavement** — the floor is *opus sectile* (cut-stone inlay), "of marvellous invention," continued
  on p. 352 (and then revealed to be, at the very centre, a single mirror of obsidian). The theatre's floor
  is its greatest marvel.
- This page and p. 325 together fully fix the centre of Cythera; the world's `_buildCytheraIsle` theatre can
  be checked against a 32-pace Area within an 8-pace ring.
