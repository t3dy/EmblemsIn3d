It happened that I gazed with parted lips most intently (my unstable and most swift eyelids not
moving), with my mind ravished, attending only to those most beautiful and so well disposed and
perfectly ordered and artificially painted and elegantly expressed histories, left inviolate by any
breakage — so tenacious was the seizing glue that the glass tesserae, pressed down page-wise and
perpetually cohering, stood to this hour unhurt, and not one removed from its placing. For that
excellent craftsman had gathered every absolute diligence to this excellent making.

And there, carrying myself foot before foot, pertinaciously examining with what direction of the
painter's art he had observed to place with pondered distribution the readiest figures upon their just
planes. And how the lines of the fabrics drew towards the object. And how from the eyes certain places
were as it were lost. And things imperfect brought little by little to the perfect, and so on the
contrary, granting his judgment to the eyes. With the exquisite by-works: waters, springs, mountains,
hills, little woods, animals. The colouring impaired with distance, and with the light opposed, and with
the neat reflections in the folds of the garments and in the other workings, not with little emulation
of shrewd nature. So wondering and absorbed that I was almost not present in myself.

In this manner I had come to the end of the entry, where the gracious histories ended, because beyond
was a most thick darkness that I did not dare to enter. But turning myself therefore to go back —
behold, without pause I heard through the broken ruins as it were a crashing of bones and of crackling
branches. I stood still, my so sweet solace suddenly interrupted and cut off.

And thereafter I heard still more plainly as it were a dragging, as of a great dead ox, through the
warty place and over the heaped and uneven ruins, always coming towards the gate with a nearer and
more consonant din; and I heard a very great hissing of an excessive serpent. I was stupefied. And my
voice being forbidden and my hair raised, I did not make myself safe by fleeing, and in that darkness
to rush on.
