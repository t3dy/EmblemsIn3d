At this most liberal invitation and royal promise and welcome, as well as I then knew how, giving her
huge thanks with devout and honourable speech, briefly and succinctly I told her point by point of my
flight from the fearful monster. And thereafter with what laborious difficulty, hurrying, I came to
this most acceptable place. At which she marvelled greatly, and with her likewise the venerable ladies.
And how besides the five companions had found me terrified and wandering.

So to such distinguished magnificence I bent my mind eagerly with the utmost pleasure. Afterwards she,
smiling charmingly, said: "It shows itself a worthy thing that an ill beginning should sometimes fall
out to a happy issue and end. And before I lay upon you anything else to carry out at present of your
loving and settled purpose and warm desire, I will that, for the profitable easing of your modest
troubles, you too should sit down with this notable company at a banquet of friendship most freely
offered — since the favouring heavens have deservedly shown you our bountiful and pious hospitality,
and have brought you beneath our triumphant roof. And therefore, my Poliphilo, without any hesitation
set yourself there to sit comfortably. For you will most gladly see part of our most splendid ordering,
and the plentiful diversity of my more than royal delights, and the chief adornment of the elegant
service, and the household splendour, and the inestimable preciousness of my most ample riches, and the
wide effect of my beneficence."

Whereupon, at this sincere and holy command, her eloquent and kindly speech being finished, I humbly
made myself her bowing servant, and with a small boldness and slight daring, obeying at once, I set
myself to sit upon those delicious benches on the right side — still in my woollen gown, which yet held
the burrs, or little burdocks, stuck in it, and some clinging husks of goose-grass — in the midst of
the five companions, placed second, between Osfressia and Achoe, after the Queen. Six other
companions on the other side. So far apart one from another that they took up that length in an
orderly way. And in the lofty middle throne, come down upon the lower step, she sat in royal fashion,
and with an august dignity.
