## 1. The finding that outranks the rest: the plate catalogue is offset

`hp.db.woodcut_catalog.page_seq` runs about **ten pages — five leaves — behind the text each
plate illustrates**. Five researchers hit it independently today and every one of them confirmed
it the same honest way: open the scan, read its printed Italian, match it verbatim against
`translation/en/`. `hp1499_p386.jpg` is our p. 396; `p421` is our p. 431; `p433` is our p. 443;
`p447` is our p. 457; `p341` carries the Area-of-32-paces passage, which is our p. 351.

`coverage_seed.py` derives `chapters[].plates` by matching `page_seq` into a chapter's page range,
so **plates are filed a chapter early throughout Book II and much of Book I**. Consequences
already in the ledger: the corpse-drag (#153) dropped from chapter XXVI altogether; #157–160 filed
under XXVIII when they illustrate XXIX; #164–165 under XXXI when they belong to XXXII–XXXIII.

**This is rule 6 wearing a third costume.** The vaults were missed because a chapter had no plate.
Chapter XI was missed because `tours.json` filed its stops elsewhere. Now: a chapter *has* a plate
and the catalogue points it at the wrong chapter — so a plate-driven check passes a chapter whose
prose never mentions what the plate shows. **Do not assume the offset is a constant ten** before
measuring it across Book I; that would trade a known error for an unknown one.

## 4. Traps — read before debugging

**New today, and it cost a wrong conclusion in this very session:**

1. **A screenshot taken during a `hpGoTo` teleport shows the camera mid-flight.** Standing at the
   dark wood, a screenshot taken 2.5 s after the call showed a void where the forest floor should
   be, and the obvious conclusion — "the spread left the wood with no ground" — was wrong. Waiting
   4 s and looking down showed the wood entirely intact. **Wait for the station panel to fire
   before believing a screenshot.**
2. **A world-position census cannot see merged geometry** (this was already known) — and it will
   quietly *support* a wrong conclusion. Traversing the live scene for large `PlaneGeometry`
   ground found only three planes and none of them under the wood, which looked like corroboration
   and was an artefact of `_compileDrawCalls` having merged them.
3. **Hand-copied constants do not move when the table they were copied from moves.** The default
   spawn was a literal `(0, 0, 448)` — the old `plain` station's `pos`, copied — in
   `HPWorldScene`'s constructor and twice more in `main.js`. Scaling `HP_STATIONS` left all three
   behind. Grep for a station's literal coordinates before trusting that moving it moved everything.
4. **When `constants.js` changes, bump every module that imports it**, not just the import line.
   (Carried over: it bit two sessions ago and again here.)
